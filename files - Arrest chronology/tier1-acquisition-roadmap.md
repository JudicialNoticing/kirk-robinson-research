## Phase 3 — GRAMA requests (file week 1; responses weeks 2–8+)

Statutory frame: Utah Code 63G-2 (GRAMA). Agencies must respond within roughly 5–10 business days (the shorter period applies where release primarily benefits the public). Request fee waivers on public-benefit grounds (63G-2-203). Denials are appealable to the agency's chief administrative officer (file within 30 days), then to the **State Records Committee** or district court. Expect the "protected records" exemption for investigation materials that could interfere with a pending proceeding (63G-2-305) to be invoked liberally while the prosecution is live — **booking and initial-contact categories are the strong plays now; investigative files are post-disposition plays.**

**3.1 Utah County Sheriff's Office (jail records) — HIGH PRIORITY, HIGH SUCCESS PROBABILITY.**
Request: complete inmate booking record for Tyler James Robinson (booked on or about Sept. 12, 2025), including booking sheet, intake/processing log with timestamps, OTN cross-reference, and booking photograph record. Booking information is a presumptively public category under GRAMA.
**Payoff:** converts the Crowder-provenance booking sheet (arrest 10:00 p.m. Sept. 11; booked 1:58 a.m. Sept. 12) into an agency-certified document — the anchor for discrepancy #2.

## Phase 5 — Verification and library protocol (applies to every acquisition)

1. **Provenance log** per document: source (XChange / clerk / agency / DocumentCloud), retrieval date, URL or request number, and whether the copy is certified, stamped, or informal.
2. **Hash and archive**: compute SHA-256 on receipt; store alongside the PDF in the project library; never annotate the original file.
3. **Tier upgrade rule**: a claim graduates from "Tier 1 via reporting" to unqualified Tier 1 only when the primary document is in the library and the claim is re-verified against its actual text — then update the relevant memo and its Revision Log.
4. **Discrepancy register discipline**: on receipt of each target document, explicitly adjudicate the register items it bears on (e.g., booking sheet → discrepancy #2; motion p. 70 → discrepancy #1; Day 3 transcript → paraphrase conflicts) and record the resolution *or* the residual conflict.
5. **Denials are records too**: file every GRAMA/FOIA denial letter; they establish existence, custodian, and the exemption claimed — and they're the predicate for appeals.
