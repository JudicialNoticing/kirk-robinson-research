# The Rifle's Path: Official Timeline of the Charlie Kirk Assassination

**How prosecutors say Tyler Robinson got the gun onto campus, onto the roof, and into the woods**

*State of Utah v. Tyler James Robinson — Compiled July 9, 2026 · Audited and updated July 13, 2026*

---

## About This Document

This timeline reconstructs the **official narrative** — the prosecution's account — of the weapon's movement on September 10–11, 2025. It is compiled from three bodies of material:

1. **The arrest affidavit / probable cause statement** (September 12, 2025) [[13]](https://www.abc4.com/news/charlie-kirk-shooting/charlie-kirk-assassination-tyler-robinson/) [[14]](https://utahnewsdispatch.com/2025/09/12/22-year-old-utahn-suspected-of-killing-charlie-kirk-in-custody/)
2. **Charging documents** filed September 16, 2025 by Utah County Attorney Jeffrey Gray, including alleged text messages between Robinson and his roommate/romantic partner, Lance Twiggs [[11]](https://www.newsweek.com/tyler-robinson-roommate-lance-twiggs-charlie-kirk-texts-2130798) [[12]](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2025/9/17/charlie-kirk-shooting-tyler-robinsons-messages-and-charges-against-him)
3. **Preliminary hearing testimony**, July 6–10, 2026, in Utah's 4th District Court (Provo, Judge Tony Graf), as reported by outlets covering the hearing. The **state's witnesses** included Utah State Bureau of Investigation agent David Hull (the lead investigator, major crimes division), UVU officer Chris Bagley, SBI Sgt. Jennifer Faumuina (who oversaw crime scenes and physical evidence), and Agent Brian Davis [[3]](https://www.kuer.org/news/2026-07-07/charlie-kirk-tyler-robinson-preliminary-hearing-day-2) [[6]](https://www.newsnationnow.com/crime/charlie-kirk-murder-suspect-dna-hearing/amp/) [[22]](https://www.abc4.com/news/charlie-kirk-shooting/tyler-robinson-hearing-resumes-new-law-enforcement-video-expected/). The **defense** called three forensic experts from the FBI and ATF — including FBI DNA analyst Amanda Bakker — to challenge the reliability of the DNA evidence [[22]](https://www.abc4.com/news/charlie-kirk-shooting/tyler-robinson-hearing-resumes-new-law-enforcement-video-expected/) [[25]](https://www.ksl.com/article/51596294/tyler-robinson-preliminary-hearing-expected-to-wrap-up-friday)

**Important status note:** These are allegations, not adjudicated facts. Robinson, 23, has not entered a plea; the preliminary hearing concluded July 10, 2026, but the probable cause determination is **pending** — briefing and oral arguments run into September (see Case Status) [[25]](https://www.ksl.com/article/51596294/tyler-robinson-preliminary-hearing-expected-to-wrap-up-friday) [[26]](https://www.fox35orlando.com/news/tyler-robinson-hearing-july-10). He is presumed innocent. Contested points, acknowledged evidentiary gaps, and source discrepancies are listed in the final sections.

## The Weapon

- **Rifle:** Mauser Model 98 bolt-action, .30-06 caliber, with a mounted scope [[10]](https://www.cnn.com/2026/07/06/us/charlie-kirk-tyler-robinson-hearing) [[15]](https://www.cnn.com/2025/09/29/us/tyler-robinson-charlie-kirk-court)
- **Provenance:** A family gun — per alleged texts, "grandpa's rifle," given to Robinson as a gift; his father later recognized it from photos released by investigators [[11]](https://www.newsweek.com/tyler-robinson-roommate-lance-twiggs-charlie-kirk-texts-2130798) [[12]](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2025/9/17/charlie-kirk-shooting-tyler-robinsons-messages-and-charges-against-him)
- **Condition when recovered:** Wrapped in a dark towel; contained one spent cartridge case and three unfired rounds, each casing engraved with meme-style messages (the fired case read "NoTices Bulge OWO What's This?"; others referenced "Hey Facist! Catch!" with arrow symbols, the anti-fascist song "Bella Ciao," and "If you Read This, You Are GAY Lmao") [[10]](https://www.cnn.com/2026/07/06/us/charlie-kirk-tyler-robinson-hearing) [[12]](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2025/9/17/charlie-kirk-shooting-tyler-robinsons-messages-and-charges-against-him). Photos of the rifle, towel, and engraved cartridges were shown in court on July 9, 2026 [[23]](https://www.newsnationnow.com/crime/charlie-kirk-suspect-roommate-center-dispute/) [[24]](https://www.abc4.com/news/charlie-kirk-shooting/tyler-robinson-preliminary-hearing-day-four/)
- **Concealment method (alleged):** Investigators contend Robinson carried the full-length rifle across campus hidden down his pant leg, producing the stiff-legged limp visible on surveillance video [[9]](https://abcnews.com/US/defense-objects-video-appearing-show-charlie-kirks-alleged/story?id=134554319)
- **Later corroboration presented in court (July 9, 2026):** Sgt. Faumuina testified investigators found shooting targets, ammunition, and a Dremel tool capable of engraving objects in Robinson's bedroom; a partially burned note in the apartment's kitchen trash; and no firearm in the home matching the recovered ammunition [[23]](https://www.newsnationnow.com/crime/charlie-kirk-suspect-roommate-center-dispute/)

## Timeline — Wednesday, September 10, 2025

### Planning window (per alleged texts and charging documents)

- Robinson allegedly told Twiggs afterward that he had been planning the attack for "a bit over a week" [[11]](https://www.newsweek.com/tyler-robinson-roommate-lance-twiggs-charlie-kirk-texts-2130798) [[12]](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2025/9/17/charlie-kirk-shooting-tyler-robinsons-messages-and-charges-against-him)
- At an unknown time on September 10, he texted Twiggs to look under his keyboard, where a note read: "I had the opportunity to take out Charlie Kirk and I'm going to take it" [[1]](https://abcnews.com/US/wireStory/timeline-charlie-kirks-assassination-arrest-tyler-robinson-134492212)

### Campus visit 1 — early morning

- **~8:29 a.m.:** Per the arrest affidavit, surveillance captures Robinson arriving at Utah Valley University in a gray Dodge Challenger, wearing a plain maroon T-shirt, light-colored shorts, a black hat with a white logo, and light-colored shoes [[13]](https://www.abc4.com/news/charlie-kirk-shooting/charlie-kirk-assassination-tyler-robinson/) [[14]](https://utahnewsdispatch.com/2025/09/12/22-year-old-utahn-suspected-of-killing-charlie-kirk-in-custody/) [[15]](https://www.cnn.com/2025/09/29/us/tyler-robinson-charlie-kirk-court)
- Video shows him walking out of a parking garage, returning about an hour later, and driving away [[2]](https://www.washingtontimes.com/news/2026/jul/7/video-evidence-tracked-alleged-charlie-kirk-assassin-carrying/)
- Surveillance across the day also captured him speaking with Turning Point USA staff and stopping for a meal [[3]](https://www.kuer.org/news/2026-07-07/charlie-kirk-tyler-robinson-preliminary-hearing-day-2)
- Investigators ultimately counted roughly **four separate campus visits** that day: twice before the shooting, around the time of the shooting, and once late that night [[4]](https://www.ksl.com/article/51594777/videos-medical-examiners-report-submitted-at-robinson-prelim-despite-flurry-of-hearsay-objections) [[5]](https://www.newsnationnow.com/crime/prosecutors-death-penalty-tyler-robinson-preliminary-hearing/)

### Campus visit 2 — return trip and clothing change (~10:00–11:00 a.m.)

- **~10:00 a.m.:** Returns **on foot**, carrying a **blue backpack**, in a short-sleeved T-shirt, shorts, and Converse sneakers [[2]](https://www.washingtontimes.com/news/2026/jul/7/video-evidence-tracked-alleged-charlie-kirk-assassin-carrying/)
- Buys and eats a meal at the campus Chick-fil-A; cameras track him moving across campus [[2]](https://www.washingtontimes.com/news/2026/jul/7/video-evidence-tracked-alleged-charlie-kirk-assassin-carrying/)
- Crosses Campus Drive into a **wooded area on its northeast side** — the same woods where the rifle would later be found [[3]](https://www.kuer.org/news/2026-07-07/charlie-kirk-tyler-robinson-preliminary-hearing-day-2) [[9]](https://abcnews.com/US/defense-objects-video-appearing-show-charlie-kirks-alleged/story?id=134554319)
- Reemerges **without the backpack**, now wearing a **long-sleeved shirt and long pants** [[2]](https://www.washingtontimes.com/news/2026/jul/7/video-evidence-tracked-alleged-charlie-kirk-assassin-carrying/) [[3]](https://www.kuer.org/news/2026-07-07/charlie-kirk-tyler-robinson-preliminary-hearing-day-2) [[9]](https://abcnews.com/US/defense-objects-video-appearing-show-charlie-kirks-alleged/story?id=134554319). Prosecutors characterize the morning movements as preparation; whether the backpack, clothes, or rifle were left in the woods at this point is not specified in public testimony
- Walks through two campus buildings to the area that gives access to the **Losee Center rooftop** — characterized by investigators as scouting the route to the firing position [[2]](https://www.washingtontimes.com/news/2026/jul/7/video-evidence-tracked-alleged-charlie-kirk-assassin-carrying/)
- **~11:00 a.m.:** Leaves campus [[2]](https://www.washingtontimes.com/news/2026/jul/7/video-evidence-tracked-alleged-charlie-kirk-assassin-carrying/)

### Campus visit 3 — the approach and the shot (11:51 a.m.–12:23 p.m.)

- **~11:51–11:52 a.m.:** Returns on foot, again carrying the backpack — now **walking with a pronounced limp**, holding his right leg straight; this is the state's evidence that the rifle was concealed in his pant leg [[1]](https://abcnews.com/US/wireStory/timeline-charlie-kirks-assassination-arrest-tyler-robinson-134492212) [[2]](https://www.washingtontimes.com/news/2026/jul/7/video-evidence-tracked-alleged-charlie-kirk-assassin-carrying/) [[7]](https://www.newsnationnow.com/crime/charlie-kirks-accused-killer-second-day-evidence-hearing/)
- Climbs the stairs of a parking structure **one step at a time**, keeping the right leg from bending, then approaches the Losee Center [[2]](https://www.washingtontimes.com/news/2026/jul/7/video-evidence-tracked-alleged-charlie-kirk-assassin-carrying/)
- **Roof access:** Per Officer Bagley's testimony, the Losee Center roof was reachable from a **public stairway**, with only a **guardrail** marking the restricted area [[5]](https://www.newsnationnow.com/crime/prosecutors-death-penalty-tyler-robinson-preliminary-hearing/). Elevated public walkways connect the Losee Center to other buildings along the roofline; at the time, a waist-high rail was the only barrier onto the roof. The access point has since been sealed with tall fencing, no-trespassing signage, and cameras [[8]](https://www.newsnationnow.com/crime/charlie-kirk-rooftop-easy-access/)
- Video shows the figure slowly climbing stairs, getting onto the roof, then **crouching and crawling toward a rooftop corner** [[3]](https://www.kuer.org/news/2026-07-07/charlie-kirk-tyler-robinson-preliminary-hearing-day-2) — specifically the **southwest corner** of the building [[2]](https://www.washingtontimes.com/news/2026/jul/7/video-evidence-tracked-alleged-charlie-kirk-assassin-carrying/)
- He lies **prone** at the corner — investigators estimate for only **15 to 30 seconds** — before firing [[6]](https://www.newsnationnow.com/crime/charlie-kirk-murder-suspect-dna-hearing/amp/) [[7]](https://www.newsnationnow.com/crime/charlie-kirks-accused-killer-second-day-evidence-hearing/)
- **12:23 p.m.:** A single .30-06 round is fired into the outdoor event below; Kirk, seated under a tent taking audience questions before roughly 3,000 people, is struck in the neck [[2]](https://www.washingtontimes.com/news/2026/jul/7/video-evidence-tracked-alleged-charlie-kirk-assassin-carrying/) [[13]](https://www.abc4.com/news/charlie-kirk-shooting/charlie-kirk-assassination-tyler-robinson/) [[28]](https://www.cnn.com/2026/07/11/us/charlie-kirk-tyler-robinson-hearing-takeaways). Charging documents put the firing distance at roughly 160 yards [[11]](https://www.newsweek.com/tyler-robinson-roommate-lance-twiggs-charlie-kirk-texts-2130798); UVU initially described the distance as about 200 yards [[20]](https://www.cnbc.com/2025/09/11/charlie-kirk-shooter-manhunt-trump.html). (On the shot's exact time, see the Source Discrepancy Register)
- Children under 14 were in view of the shooting — the basis of one charged count [[11]](https://www.newsweek.com/tyler-robinson-roommate-lance-twiggs-charlie-kirk-texts-2130798) [[12]](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2025/9/17/charlie-kirk-shooting-tyler-robinsons-messages-and-charges-against-him)

### The escape — getting the rifle off the roof (12:23 p.m. onward)

- **Seconds after the shot:** The same figure — notably **no longer limping** — moves back across the roof [[3]](https://www.kuer.org/news/2026-07-07/charlie-kirk-tyler-robinson-preliminary-hearing-day-2)
- He reaches the **north end of the roof** — the side away from the crowd — **lowers himself off the edge and drops to the ground** [[2]](https://www.washingtontimes.com/news/2026/jul/7/video-evidence-tracked-alleged-charlie-kirk-assassin-carrying/) [[4]](https://www.ksl.com/article/51594777/videos-medical-examiners-report-submitted-at-robinson-prelim-despite-flurry-of-hearsay-objections). Investigators later found a **shoe impression on the northeast corner of the rooftop edge**, near where the suspect climbed down [[13]](https://www.abc4.com/news/charlie-kirk-shooting/charlie-kirk-assassination-tyler-robinson/)
- He hurries away carrying an object [[2]](https://www.washingtontimes.com/news/2026/jul/7/video-evidence-tracked-alleged-charlie-kirk-assassin-carrying/). (On cross-examination, Hull conceded no firearm is actually visible in any video — only "a long object consistent with a concealed rifle" [[6]](https://www.newsnationnow.com/crime/charlie-kirk-murder-suspect-dna-hearing/amp/))
- He **crosses Campus Drive** into the northeast wooded area; Hull testified no drivers reported seeing anyone carrying a gun [[3]](https://www.kuer.org/news/2026-07-07/charlie-kirk-tyler-robinson-preliminary-hearing-day-2) [[7]](https://www.newsnationnow.com/crime/charlie-kirks-accused-killer-second-day-evidence-hearing/)
- **In the woods:** The state alleges he wrapped the rifle in a towel and left it in a bush, then walked off campus. His alleged texts to Twiggs corroborate this: "I had to leave it in a bush where I changed outfits. Didn't have the ability or time to bring it with... Only thing I left was the rifle wrapped in a towel" [[11]](https://www.newsweek.com/tyler-robinson-roommate-lance-twiggs-charlie-kirk-texts-2130798). The arrest affidavit likewise describes messages about "leaving the rifle in a bush" and a rifle "wrapped in a towel" [[13]](https://www.abc4.com/news/charlie-kirk-shooting/charlie-kirk-assassination-tyler-robinson/). The FBI's public case summary states the shooter "left a gun and ammunition in a wooded area near the university" [[19]](https://www.fbi.gov/news/press-releases/utah-valley-shooting-updates)

### Why no one caught it in the moment (per testimony and reporting)

- **A false confession pulled attention groundward:** Moments after the shot — within about 30 seconds by Officer Bagley's estimate [[4]](https://www.ksl.com/article/51594777/videos-medical-examiners-report-submitted-at-robinson-prelim-despite-flurry-of-hearsay-objections), roughly two minutes per police reports [[16]](https://www.deseret.com/politics/2026/01/29/george-zinn-pleads-guilty-three-counts-charlie-kirk/) — officers detained **George Zinn**, 71, who shouted "I shot him, now shoot me" despite having no weapon and no involvement [[16]](https://www.deseret.com/politics/2026/01/29/george-zinn-pleads-guilty-three-counts-charlie-kirk/). Video of Zinn's arrest spread rapidly online, leading many to believe the shooter was caught; his false confession diverted officers who could have been securing the scene or hunting the actual shooter [[17]](https://www.ksl.com/article/51440384/utah-man-who-falsely-claimed-he-shot-charlie-kirk-sentenced-to-prison). (Zinn later pleaded no contest to obstruction of justice [[16]](https://www.deseret.com/politics/2026/01/29/george-zinn-pleads-guilty-three-counts-charlie-kirk/).) UVU even issued a 12:47 p.m. alert stating the suspect was in custody; authorities corrected this by late afternoon, and FBI Director Kash Patel's "subject in custody" post at 4:21 p.m. was followed at 5:59 p.m. by an announcement that the person had been released [[1]](https://abcnews.com/US/wireStory/timeline-charlie-kirks-assassination-arrest-tyler-robinson-134492212)
- **Thin security:** Six officers were working an event with roughly 3,000 attendees [[5]](https://www.newsnationnow.com/crime/prosecutors-death-penalty-tyler-robinson-preliminary-hearing/) [[28]](https://www.cnn.com/2026/07/11/us/charlie-kirk-tyler-robinson-hearing-takeaways); there were no metal detectors and no drones, and Bagley testified he attended no pre-event security planning meeting [[5]](https://www.newsnationnow.com/crime/prosecutors-death-penalty-tyler-robinson-preliminary-hearing/)
- **Open roofline:** The guardrail-only access from a public walkway meant reaching the roof required no forced entry [[5]](https://www.newsnationnow.com/crime/prosecutors-death-penalty-tyler-robinson-preliminary-hearing/) [[8]](https://www.newsnationnow.com/crime/charlie-kirk-rooftop-easy-access/)

### The rooftop scene (12:44 p.m.)

- Officer Bagley reaches the Losee roof and finds a **red-and-black screwdriver** and a disturbance in the gravel he described as a "sniper pad" — impressions of elbows, knees, and feet in a direct line of sight to Kirk's tent [[4]](https://www.ksl.com/article/51594777/videos-medical-examiners-report-submitted-at-robinson-prelim-despite-flurry-of-hearsay-objections) [[5]](https://www.newsnationnow.com/crime/prosecutors-death-penalty-tyler-robinson-preliminary-hearing/)
- Per the FBI's public case updates, trace evidence collected from the rooftop scene included **shoe impressions, a forearm imprint, and a palm print** [[19]](https://www.fbi.gov/news/press-releases/utah-valley-shooting-updates)
- **No shell casing or bullet was found on or around the roof** [[6]](https://www.newsnationnow.com/crime/charlie-kirk-murder-suspect-dna-hearing/amp/). (The state's reconciliation — an inference consistent with the recovered evidence rather than a directly testified fact: a bolt-action retains the fired case unless the bolt is cycled, and the rifle was recovered containing one spent round and three unspent [[15]](https://www.cnn.com/2025/09/29/us/tyler-robinson-charlie-kirk-court) [[23]](https://www.newsnationnow.com/crime/charlie-kirk-suspect-roommate-center-dispute/))

## Timeline — Thursday, September 11, 2025

### The retrieval attempt (after midnight)

- Robinson allegedly returns to campus a **fourth time** in the early hours, briefly encountering a police officer working campus security; his car is recorded leaving campus after midnight [[2]](https://www.washingtontimes.com/news/2026/jul/7/video-evidence-tracked-alleged-charlie-kirk-assassin-carrying/) [[3]](https://www.kuer.org/news/2026-07-07/charlie-kirk-tyler-robinson-preliminary-hearing-day-2) [[7]](https://www.newsnationnow.com/crime/charlie-kirks-accused-killer-second-day-evidence-hearing/)
- His alleged texts describe the motive for returning: "If I am able to grab my rifle unseen, I will have left no evidence." He reports he could get close, but a squad car was parked near the spot, and ultimately: "I might have to abandon it and hope they don't find prints" [[11]](https://www.newsweek.com/tyler-robinson-roommate-lance-twiggs-charlie-kirk-texts-2130798) [[1]](https://abcnews.com/US/wireStory/timeline-charlie-kirks-assassination-arrest-tyler-robinson-134492212)

### Recovery, identification, surrender

- **By Thursday morning:** FBI Special Agent in Charge Robert Bohls announces investigators traced the shooter's escape route and recovered a high-powered bolt-action rifle from the wooded area [[20]](https://www.cnbc.com/2025/09/11/charlie-kirk-shooter-manhunt-trump.html) [[21]](https://www.foxnews.com/us/person-interest-charlie-kirk-assassination-pictured-photos-released-fbi). Sgt. Faumuina later testified to finding the Mauser wrapped in the dark towel there, photographing the scene, clearing the ammunition, and packaging the evidence for the FBI and ATF labs [[7]](https://www.newsnationnow.com/crime/charlie-kirks-accused-killer-second-day-evidence-hearing/) [[22]](https://www.abc4.com/news/charlie-kirk-shooting/tyler-robinson-hearing-resumes-new-law-enforcement-video-expected/) [[23]](https://www.newsnationnow.com/crime/charlie-kirk-suspect-roommate-center-dispute/)
- **Morning/midday:** The FBI releases two photos of a person of interest (hat, sunglasses, long-sleeve black shirt) and announces a $100,000 reward [[20]](https://www.cnbc.com/2025/09/11/charlie-kirk-shooter-manhunt-trump.html) [[21]](https://www.foxnews.com/us/person-interest-charlie-kirk-assassination-pictured-photos-released-fbi)
- **Evening:** Per the FBI's own updates page, four additional, clearer photos are released at 9:05 p.m. ET (~7 p.m. MT), followed at 10 p.m. ET by video of the shooter jumping from the rooftop [[19]](https://www.fbi.gov/news/press-releases/utah-valley-shooting-updates); the AP timeline logs the person-of-interest release at 7:55 p.m. local time [[1]](https://abcnews.com/US/wireStory/timeline-charlie-kirks-assassination-arrest-tyler-robinson-134492212). Video shown at the evening press conference tracks the suspect crossing the roof, dropping down the side of the building, and crossing a street into the wooded area where the rifle was recovered [[21]](https://www.foxnews.com/us/person-interest-charlie-kirk-assassination-pictured-photos-released-fbi)
- Robinson's mother recognizes him from the released images — which had circulated since midday — and his parents confront him [[1]](https://abcnews.com/US/wireStory/timeline-charlie-kirks-assassination-arrest-tyler-robinson-134492212); his father separately recognizes the rifle in released photos as the one gifted to his son [[11]](https://www.newsweek.com/tyler-robinson-roommate-lance-twiggs-charlie-kirk-texts-2130798). Robinson allegedly tells his parents Kirk "spreads too much hate" [[11]](https://www.newsweek.com/tyler-robinson-roommate-lance-twiggs-charlie-kirk-texts-2130798)
- **8:02 p.m.:** A family friend — a retired sheriff's deputy — calls Washington County Sheriff Nate Brooksby, saying he knows the shooter's identity and is working with the family on a voluntary surrender [[1]](https://abcnews.com/US/wireStory/timeline-charlie-kirks-assassination-arrest-tyler-robinson-134492212)
- **~9:00 p.m.** (roughly 33 hours after the shooting): Robinson turns himself in with his parents at the Washington County Sheriff's Office [[1]](https://abcnews.com/US/wireStory/timeline-charlie-kirks-assassination-arrest-tyler-robinson-134492212) [[6]](https://www.newsnationnow.com/crime/charlie-kirk-murder-suspect-dna-hearing/amp/)
- **~4:00 a.m. September 12:** He is formally arrested and taken to the Utah County Jail, where clothing, fingerprints, and DNA are collected under a search warrant [[6]](https://www.newsnationnow.com/crime/charlie-kirk-murder-suspect-dna-hearing/amp/); authorities publicly announce the arrest at 8:05 a.m. [[1]](https://abcnews.com/US/wireStory/timeline-charlie-kirks-assassination-arrest-tyler-robinson-134492212)

### Charges (filed September 16, 2025)

Aggravated murder (a capital offense — the state is seeking the death penalty), felony discharge of a firearm, obstruction of justice (for moving and concealing the rifle, and separately for disposing of clothing), witness tampering (for allegedly telling Twiggs to delete their messages and stay silent), and committing a violent offense in the presence of a child [[10]](https://www.cnn.com/2026/07/06/us/charlie-kirk-tyler-robinson-hearing) [[12]](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2025/9/17/charlie-kirk-shooting-tyler-robinsons-messages-and-charges-against-him). As of the July 2026 preliminary hearing, he faces ten charges in total [[4]](https://www.ksl.com/article/51594777/videos-medical-examiners-report-submitted-at-robinson-prelim-despite-flurry-of-hearsay-objections)

## Gaps, Caveats, and Defense Challenges (as of the hearing's July 10, 2026 conclusion)

The following points come from cross-examination, defense filings, and the state's own witnesses:

- **No casing or bullet on the roof.** Consistent with the state's bolt-action explanation, but the defense has highlighted the absence of physical ballistic evidence at the firing position itself [[6]](https://www.newsnationnow.com/crime/charlie-kirk-murder-suspect-dna-hearing/amp/)
- **No firearm visible on video.** Hull acknowledged investigators never saw a gun in the surveillance footage — only a long object consistent with a concealed rifle [[6]](https://www.newsnationnow.com/crime/charlie-kirk-murder-suspect-dna-hearing/amp/)
- **No eyewitness to the gun.** No drivers or bystanders reported seeing a weapon as the suspect crossed Campus Drive [[7]](https://www.newsnationnow.com/crime/charlie-kirks-accused-killer-second-day-evidence-hearing/)
- **Ballistics are inconclusive.** A federal analysis of the bullet fragments recovered from Kirk's body could neither identify nor exclude the recovered Mauser as the source, per court documents [[10]](https://www.cnn.com/2026/07/06/us/charlie-kirk-tyler-robinson-hearing)
- **DNA is probabilistic, not absolute — and note who said what.** Charging documents said DNA "consistent with" Robinson's was on the trigger, fired casing, two unfired cartridges, and towel [[10]](https://www.cnn.com/2026/07/06/us/charlie-kirk-tyler-robinson-hearing) [[15]](https://www.cnn.com/2025/09/29/us/tyler-robinson-charlie-kirk-court). At the hearing, it was **prosecution witness Sgt. Faumuina** who read the FBI report's findings into the record: male DNA on the towel interpreted as a two-person mixture from Robinson and Twiggs, with the results more likely if both contributed than if Twiggs and an unknown person had [[7]](https://www.newsnationnow.com/crime/charlie-kirks-accused-killer-second-day-evidence-hearing/). The **defense** then called FBI DNA analyst Amanda Bakker — one of three FBI/ATF forensic experts called by the defense [[22]](https://www.abc4.com/news/charlie-kirk-shooting/tyler-robinson-hearing-resumes-new-law-enforcement-video-expected/) [[25]](https://www.ksl.com/article/51596294/tyler-robinson-preliminary-hearing-expected-to-wrap-up-friday) — who testified the testing did not identify Robinson as *the source* but that he could not be excluded as a *possible contributor* to the towel and screwdriver DNA [[6]](https://www.newsnationnow.com/crime/charlie-kirk-murder-suspect-dna-hearing/amp/) [[18]](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/nation/dna-evidence-from-charlie-kirk-assassination-disputed-by-defendants-lawyers). Bakker noted FBI policy bars analysts from ever describing a result as an "absolute identification" or error-free [[7]](https://www.newsnationnow.com/crime/charlie-kirks-accused-killer-second-day-evidence-hearing/) — so the hedged phrasing reflects mandated reporting convention as well as the underlying statistics. Defense filings separately note some items showed DNA from up to "five or more" people [[10]](https://www.cnn.com/2026/07/06/us/charlie-kirk-tyler-robinson-hearing)
- **The suspect was not independently identified.** Defense attorneys emphasized repeatedly that Robinson was not on investigators' radar until he surrendered [[6]](https://www.newsnationnow.com/crime/charlie-kirk-murder-suspect-dna-hearing/amp/) [[25]](https://www.ksl.com/article/51596294/tyler-robinson-preliminary-hearing-expected-to-wrap-up-friday)
- **The video compilation is edited.** The defense contests the authenticity of the surveillance compilation played in court, noting it was altered/assembled by the prosecution [[5]](https://www.newsnationnow.com/crime/prosecutors-death-penalty-tyler-robinson-preliminary-hearing/) [[9]](https://abcnews.com/US/defense-objects-video-appearing-show-charlie-kirks-alleged/story?id=134554319)
- **Key witness immunity.** Twiggs cooperated and provided the text messages under limited-use immunity from state and federal prosecutors [[6]](https://www.newsnationnow.com/crime/charlie-kirk-murder-suspect-dna-hearing/amp/)

## Source Discrepancy Register

The counts reconcile, but the framings differ.
7. **Surrender-to-booking window:** ~9 p.m. arrival at the sheriff's office per the AP [[1]](https://abcnews.com/US/wireStory/timeline-charlie-kirks-assassination-arrest-tyler-robinson-134492212) and formal arrest ~4 a.m. per Agent Davis's testimony [[6]](https://www.newsnationnow.com/crime/charlie-kirk-murder-suspect-dna-hearing/amp/) are compatible, but accounts of exactly what happened in that window vary across source types.

## Case Status (as of July 13, 2026)

The five-day preliminary hearing ran Monday–Friday, July 6–10, 2026 [[27]](https://www.deseret.com/politics/2026/07/10/tyler-robinson-preliminary-hearing-charlie-kirk-assassination-utah-court/). Robinson, on his attorneys' recommendation, declined to testify [[25]](https://www.ksl.com/article/51596294/tyler-robinson-preliminary-hearing-expected-to-wrap-up-friday) [[26]](https://www.fox35orlando.com/news/tyler-robinson-hearing-july-10).
