# SEC-E02 — Allegations against Charlie Kirk's security detail: what is claimed, and what the record carries

**File ID:** SEC-E02
**Scope:** the online allegation that a member of Charlie Kirk's private security detail shot him — the *"palm pistol"* claim — and the associated *"Israeli/Mossad security team"* framing. Records both claims faithfully, then tests them against the Tier 1 record this library holds. **Also records the security chief's own public account of the detail's conduct**, which is the only sourced first-person material on the subject.
**Case context:** *State of Utah v. Tyler James Robinson*, No. 251403576, Fourth Judicial District Court, Provo.
**Prepared:** 2026-08-17 · **Status:** **v1.0 · `[PROVISIONAL]`** — no independent verification cycle has run on this file.

> 🚨 **CASE POSTURE, as of 2026-08-17.** **Tyler Robinson has NOT been bound over.** The preliminary hearing is **unfinished**: 2026-07-06 → 07-10 were five days **of evidence**; **2026-09-01 is its last day, argument only, on a closed evidentiary record.** Presumption of innocence is absolute.
>
> 🚨 **NO MEMBER OF THE SECURITY DETAIL IS ACCUSED OF ANYTHING BY ANY OFFICIAL BODY.** No charge, no investigative finding, no filing and no official statement in this library's holdings alleges that any member of Mr. Kirk's security detail shot him, was armed with a concealed pistol, or bore any responsibility for his death. **The allegation assessed here is an internet claim. It is recorded because recording a claim is how this project tests one — never because it has standing.**

## §1 · Access path — read this before §4

⚠️ **The decisive evidentiary limit, stated first because it bounds every conclusion below.**

🚨 **THIS LIBRARY HOLDS NO WOUND-CHARACTERISTICS EVIDENCE OF ANY KIND.** **State's Exhibit 11 — the Utah medical examiner's report of examination, 9 pp., Dr. Guajardo — was ADMITTED into evidence on 2026-07-06 and PUBLICATION WAS DENIED.** The autopsy report was expressly not published; the parties on the record discussed "the non-publication of the autopsy report." **This project has never read it.**

**Consequences, and they cut in both directions:**
- ❌ **This file CANNOT say that wound morphology excludes a close-range shot.** No range-of-fire evidence — stippling, soot, powder deposition, entrance/exit characteristics — exists anywhere in the holdings. - ❌ **And the allegation has no evidentiary basis whatever.** It rests on visual interpretation of video clips. Nothing in the held record supports it, and the affirmative record set out at §4 runs squarely against it.

## §3 · The security chief's public account — the only sourced first-person material

**Brian Harpole**, head of Mr. Kirk's security detail for the American Comeback Tour, gave an interview to the **Shawn Ryan Show, episode #254**, premiered **2025-11-17** — described by the programme as breaking his silence roughly two months after the killing, and expressly covering the conspiracy theories. Reported by **Deseret News / KSL, 2025-11-19 (Eva Terry)**. **Transcript read directly via Singju Post, retrieved 2026-08-17.** **[T3 — a named principal's first-person account, uncertified transcript]**

🚨 **He is a claimant with an obvious interest in defending his own team. This is the recording layer: attributed, not adopted.**

**On the detail's conduct at the moment of the shot:**
> *"I've got my back to Charlie about, I don't know, less than 10 meters off to his right… I heard the gunfire and then I heard that bullet slap him… I turned and when I turned, started running."*
> *"I jumped down on top of Charlie to cover him… I immediately shoved my hand into the wound to stop the bleeding… I was on him within five seconds and had my hands in him."*
> *"You see our team actually doing exactly, falling back on top of him and collapsing back on top of him. **Not one radio comms came out.**"*

**On the man at the centre of C-1, by name, in the security chief's own words:**
> *"Rick breaks off at the perfect time. He goes around to the other side, jumps in."* (the exfil)
> *"**Rick has me across the kit in my shirt so that I can use both hands to do the medical one.**"*
> *"Rick and I had it all over our arms, and our, from about here down was just covered."*

**On the transport:**
> *"I'm on my knees with the door open with my butt hanging out of the side, and I'm on my knees doing the medical one… Justin's driving… we're going 60, 80, 100."* He describes packing the wound during the drive.

**On the allegations themselves:**
> *"It doesn't even have to be true, and it doesn't matter. **Palm gun exploding, microphone, hand signals.**"*
> On C-2: *"**We're Americans, period.** We believe in what we're afforded to do here… To say that we're Mossad or attached to any type of foreign entity, it's one of those sits crap on its best day."*

### 4.1 ✅ What the sworn record independently establishes

| **h** | *"[T]wo security personnel in front and diagonal to Mr. Kirk"* appear in State's Exhibit 8; *"[o]ne of Mr. Kirk's security guards comes into frame from the right of Mr. Kirk three seconds after the shot"* | Defence preliminary-hearing briefing, 2026-08-11 | **T1** *(for what was filed)* |

### 4.2 🚨 The convergence that matters most

**Hull's sworn testimony (g) and Harpole's podcast account (§3) describe the same event from outside and inside, and they agree.** Hull, under oath, from the investigative record: the detail removed Kirk while administering first aid, in a hasty transport. Harpole, in his own words: he packed the wound in the moving vehicle while *"Rick has me across the kit in my shirt so that I can use both hands."*

✅ **On the security chief's account, the man at the centre of C-1 was physically holding him into the vehicle so that both of Harpole's hands were free to treat the wound.** That is an account of the transport phase, and it is corroborated in outline by sworn Tier 1 testimony from a source with no connection to the podcast.

⚠️ **STATE THE LIMIT PRECISELY, because this is where an assessment like this usually overreaches.** C-1 concerns **hand movements in clips at or near the moment of the shot, in the courtyard.** Harpole's account of Rick is mostly about the **exfil and the van**, which are *later*. He places him in the exfil sequence (*"breaks off at the perfect time"*) but does not narrate his position at the instant of the shot. **So §4.2 answers the transport phase directly and the courtyard phase only in part.** It is corroboration, not a demonstration.

### 4.3 ⚠️ The empty holster — recorded with its context, because omitting it would be the over-hedge defect

An **empty pistol holster** was on the grass in the courtyard, seen by Officer Bagley as he cleared the scene. **This file records it, and records what the same testimony does with it: the holster is what prompted him to articulate that the shot was a rifle, not a pistol** (§4.1 d — the holster and the realisation are consecutive sentences in his testimony).

⚠️ **Its provenance is associated on the record with an off-duty trooper on scene, but the identification is NOT established.** Defence counsel put to Agent Hull that a named individual was *"an off-duty, I think maybe, trooper that was on the scene that day and that was involved in recovering the holster."* **Hull did not adopt it:** *"I know that a trooper was interviewed, having been on scene. I don't recall if it was Gonzalez. I don't recall the name."* 🚨 **A questioner's premise is not an answer.** What is established: a trooper was on scene and was interviewed. **The name and the holster-recovery role are counsel's assertion only, and this file does not adopt them.**

✅ **Calibration:** Utah is an **open-carry state** (Bagley, `day1.txt` ~02:46:25), the event was outdoors on a public campus, and a member of Mr. Kirk's detail on the Hall of Flags was **armed and known to be armed** by the officer standing next to him. **A holster in that setting is unremarkable. It is recorded because a file that suppressed it would be less trustworthy, not more.**

### 4.4 Resolution

> ✅ **C-1 (the "palm pistol" claim) is RESOLVED AGAINST, at Medium-high — and the grade is deliberately not higher.**
>
> **What carries it:** a charged and testified theory of a single rifle shot from ~160 yards (a, b); a `.30-06` cartridge tied to the firearm (c); a sworn officer distinguishing a rifle report from a pistol report contemporaneously and giving his acoustic basis (d); the only other firearm on scene accounted for in a backpack (f); sworn testimony that the detail's role was extraction and first aid (g); and the security chief's first-person account, which is corroborated in outline by (g) (§4.2).
>
> **Why not High:** 🚨 **the medical examiner's report is admitted and unpublished, so this project holds no wound evidence at all** (§1). The refutation rests on the surrounding record and not on the one document that would be decisive. **A grade must trace to a verification act, and the decisive verification act has not been available to this project.**
>
> **What would change it:** State's Exhibit 11, read directly. A grade of High is available the moment it is.

> ✅ **C-2 (the "Israeli/Mossad security team" framing) is RESOLVED AGAINST at High as to its evidentiary content, which is nil.** No source in this library's holdings — no filing, no sworn testimony, no official statement — makes any claim about the nationality, foreign affiliation or sponsorship of Mr. Kirk's detail. The claim is denied in terms by the detail's head (§3). **It carries no evidence and is recorded only as a fact about the claim environment.**

> ⚠️ **C-3 is NOT ASSESSED.** The *"exploding microphone"* and *"hand signals"* variants are recorded as circulating and named by Harpole, but this project has not examined the source material for either and **states no view.** — *(checkable)*

## §5 · Why this file is scoped as it is — a court-record basis, not an editorial preference

A sworn proffer filed **2026-02-02** by a Deputy Utah County Attorney (**docket 368**), held on disk and read directly, states at **¶6**:

> *"Members of Charlie Kirk's security team have reported threats, doxing – the publication of personal information online for harassment purposes, and risks to them since Charlie Kirk's murder."*

and at **¶5** that witnesses *"have received threats because of their involvement. Some have relocated to avoid these threats. Many have deleted online accounts and changed usernames and social media handles."* **[T1 — for what the State represented; ⚠️ a party's proffer, not an adjudicated finding]**

🚨 **This is why SEC-E02 assesses a claim rather than profiling a man.** The project rule already forbids identifying information about uninvolved private individuals *even when sources publish it*; here the court record independently establishes that publication of exactly this material is a reported, ongoing harm to this exact population. ✅ **The protective act available to a research library is not silence — it is putting the evidentiary posture on the record**, which is what §4 does.

## §6 · Findings

- **F-112 — THE DECISIVE DOCUMENT IS ADMITTED AND UNREAD, AND THAT BOUNDS EVERY CLAIM IN THIS AREA IN BOTH DIRECTIONS.** State's Exhibit 11, the ME report, was admitted 2026-07-06 with publication denied. Measured nulls across all five hearing days plus the Information and affidavit: `entrance wound`/`exit wound`/`entry wound` **0**, `stippl` **0**, `single gunshot wound` **0**. **No range-of-fire evidence is held.** Neither the allegation nor its refutation can be settled on wound morphology by this project. **Confidence: High.**
- **F-113 — THE ONLY SOURCED FIRST-PERSON ACCOUNT OF THE SUBJECT OF C-1 DESCRIBES HIM ASSISTING THE LIFE-SAVING EFFORT**, and it is corroborated in outline by sworn Tier 1 testimony about the same phase (§4.2). ⚠️ **It addresses the transport directly and the courtyard only in part. Confidence: Medium-high** *(a party's account; uncertified transcript; the podcast itself unheld)*.
- **F-114 — A CONTEMPORANEOUS ACOUSTIC DISCRIMINATION IS ON THE SWORN RECORD.** The officer nearest the courtyard identified the report as a rifle rather than a pistol *in the moment*, stated his basis, and had it assented to by his chief — **before any investigation, and with an empty holster in view.** ⚠️ It is a trained ear, not an instrument. **Confidence: High as to what was said; Medium as to the discrimination's reliability.**
- **F-115 — THE HOLSTER'S PROVENANCE IS COUNSEL'S ASSERTION, NOT EVIDENCE.** A trooper was on scene and was interviewed (established); the name and the holster-recovery role were put by counsel and **not adopted** by the witness. **Confidence: High** *(as to the state of the record)*.
- ⚠️ **F-116 — A THIRD DISTANCE FIGURE IS NOW IN CIRCULATION.** The Information says *"approximately 160 yards"*; the library's timeline file already registers a **160-vs-200-yard** conflict; open-web summaries of this case state **142 yards**. **Three renderings.** Not assigned a D-number: the divergence is between a primary document and unreliable secondaries, and nothing in this library rests on the secondary figure. **Cite the Information.**
