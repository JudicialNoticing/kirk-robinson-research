## 0. Sourcing framework

**Tier 1** sworn testimony / primary filings · **Tier 2** named official statements · **Tier 3** named journalism · **Tier 4** anonymous or low-verification. Sworn testimony is **Tier 1 as to what the witness said**, never automatically as to the world. Standing rules apply: a quotation by an interested party is reliable as to the words, not the document (CR-ACQ §0); **a forensic report is silent about the provenance of what it examined** (CR-AUDIT §4).

## 3. OFFICER CHRISTOPHER BAGLEY — A WITNESS NEW TO THIS LIBRARY

**Who he is.** Christopher Bagley; **26 years a police officer**; Utah POST academy (~600 hours), certified. On **2025-09-10** he was a senior officer with the **Utah Valley University Police Department** (five years); previously Utah County Sheriff's Office (13 years), Springville City PD (6), Salt Lake County Sheriff's Office (1), with K-9, SWAT, motors, FTO and detective roles. **He did SWAT but expressly was not a sniper** — "I worked well with them, but I was not a sniper." At the time of testimony he had been with **Spanish Fork PD about five months**.

**Why he matters.** He was **very familiar with UVU's camera system**, using it daily to trace people across campus, and had personally built clickable campus maps for dispatch. **He was on duty in the courtyard when Kirk was shot, and he was the first officer onto the Losee roof.**

### 3.1 The shooting, as he saw and heard it

- He worked an **overtime shift**, checking in about **11:00 a.m.**, posted at the south end of the Hall of Flags.
- He saw Kirk arrive, do a meet-and-greet and photographs, then sit under **the white tent** in the courtyard taking questions.
- A little after noon: "**A kid asked him a question, and then I heard a shot fired.**" He puts it at **12:23**. He could see only the right side of Kirk's body under the tent; **simultaneous with the shot** Kirk leaned to the left and went down.
- He ran down the outside stairs "**jumping over people lying on the steps**." At the bottom he was told on the radio "**we had a shooter in custody**" — within roughly a minute. His reaction: "**Oh, that was kind of fast.**"
- Reaching the tent, "**Charlie was gone. They put him in an SUV and left.**"

### 3.2 The realisation, and the empty holster

While clearing people off the tiered courtyard grass to preserve evidence, standing beside **Chief Long**, he saw an **empty concealed-pistol holster** on the top grass tier. "**That's when things started slowing down for me a little bit.**"

**In that pause he concluded the shot had been a rifle, not a handgun** — a handgun is "a short bang pop sound," a rifle "a longer violent bang or crackle sound," typically louder. He knew the sound had come from the east, and **the Losee building "was right in my" line of sight.**

⚠ **The holster was never collected.** He noticed no markings, has "**no idea**" what happened to it, never took custody of it, never told anyone else to, and "**just left it on the grass**." He does not know whether it was ever fingerprinted, or whether any other guns were confiscated on scene that day. **Recorded at the recording layer; no inference is drawn. It is an item of potential evidentiary interest, in a homicide, expressly left in place by the officer who found it.**

### 3.3 The Losee roof — first officer up, at 12:44

Route: into the Sorenson Center, up four flights to the Losee fourth level, then the outside public stairs in the breezeway between the computer-science building and the Losee. He **hopped the guardrail** and immediately saw an out-of-place object about **10–15 feet in from the guardrail**: **a red and black screwdriver** (State's Exhibit 3.1, evidence tag 5).

On the **southwest** side he found **a disturbance in the gravel**. Describing the nighttime photograph: "**to me, it looks like a sniper pad, a person that has been [lying there]**." ⚠ **His own qualification is preserved:** of the daytime photograph he said it "**does not do it justice**" — "**I saw a distinct impression in the gravel and this does not show that.**" The naked eye showed more than the exhibit does.

**He then did the thing that matters most.** Being "around guns and snipers," he **specifically looked for a spent shell casing** — at the prone position, over the edge, "all over" — and "**As a matter of fact, no**," he could not find one. **He also found no unfired bullets on the roof.**

> **Analyst layer.** This **independently corroborates**, from a different witness on a different day, CR-E04 §5.1's finding that **no bullets or casings were found on or immediately around the Losee building**. CR-E02 §4 reached it as an inference; CR-E04 raised it to Hull's direct testimony; **Bagley's is now a second, first-hand, purposive search with the same result** — and it is the strongest form of the finding, because he was **looking for one specifically and says why he knew to look.** **Confidence: High** as to the testimony. It sharpens **D-48**: the only cartridge case identified to the rifle came from **inside the rifle's chamber** (CR-E02 §4), and the perch produced nothing.

He secured the roof with tape around the southwest portion, left at about **1:01–1:03 p.m.**, and handed off. He **did not walk through or touch** the gravel disturbance or, later, the shoe print.

### 3.4 The camera system, the route, and the northeast drop

Having found a prone impression in the direct line of sight, he "**realized that we probably didn't have our shooter in custody**" and asked dispatch to check the cameras.

⚠ **Dispatch's answer was admitted NOT for its truth.** Dispatch reported a male on the roof at the time of the shooting who "**ran to the edge, dropped down, crawled, got in a prone position**," then after the shot stood and ran northeast. **The court overruled the defence hearsay objection with an express limiting finding** (02:36:05): sitting **as a magistrate, not before a jury**, "**I am not accepting that statement for the truth of the matter asserted**" — only for its effect on the hearer. Defence counsel put on the record that the State was "**clearly offering it for the truth**." **Through this witness, the dispatch account is not substantive evidence of anyone's movements.**

**What is first-hand** is that he then went to the UVU police department and "**look[ed] at the footage to verify for myself exactly what [t]ranspired**," seeing an individual run to the edge, get up, run off, and **drop off the northeast side** of the Losee building.

At the northeast drop point he found **a shoe imprint "embedded pretty deep" in the grass**, and above it on the concrete roof edge "**scratching or rub marks… that was not there / looked abnormal**." He taped the area off and released it to two Payson officers.

⚠ **What he could not see on the video:** he could **not** identify the individual's **face, clothing markings, height, shoes or headwear** — only "he wasn't heavyset… more of a skinnier person." He **saw "a long black object in the left hand"** as the person crossed the roof; asked what it was, he said "**It looks like a rifle or something to that effect, a long object… like skinny and long**." ⚠ **His report mentions neither a gun nor the object**, and he had seen the video **only twice, most recently months before testifying.**

### 3.5 Cross-examination — the most defence-favourable stretch of Day 1

Recorded in full because the both-directions rule requires it, and because a State-focused summary would drop all of it.

**Event security, before the shot:**
- **No briefing, no debrief, no meeting** that morning; he does not know whether any security meeting occurred; **no operational or tactical plan, no written materials.**
- **UVU employs 15 officers; six, including him, covered the event.** They "had an idea" thousands would attend.
- **No magnetometers.** **No drones** over campus before the shooting. UVU had no drone capability at all — one had to be brought in afterwards.
- **Nobody was ever assigned to cover the Losee building**, and he does not know if anyone was. When he reached the roof **there was no law enforcement on the roof, the stairway or the walkway.**
- Utah is an **open-carry** state. He saw nobody armed but law enforcement — though **Kirk's private security team was present and armed**, one member on the Hall of Flags beside him before the shooting.

**Evidence integrity, after the shot:**
- **His body camera died on the roof, before the tape went up.** The **27-minute-35-second** recording is his only footage of the day; he never docked or restarted it — "too chaotic."
- **He never reviewed the video for the window between the individual leaving the roof and his own arrival**, and **other people were on the roof during that window**, "looking for an individual that ran that way… clearing the building."
- **An unidentified armed man in civilian clothing accompanied him up to the roof** — visible on his body camera, wearing a badge and carrying a handgun. **Bagley does not know who he was or what agency he was from.**
- **The wrong person was detained.** Someone was taken into custody within ~30 seconds, "right down by where Charlie Kirk was sitting by his tent" — in the courtyard, not near the holster. Asked why the mismatch with the shot's direction did not register: "**During [the chaos]…**"
- **Exhibit provenance was outside his knowledge.** He does not know who took Exhibit 35, where it came from, or how old it is. He first saw Exhibits 1, 2 and 3 "**a couple of weeks ago**," in preparation for testifying.
- **The nighttime rooftop photographs rest on hearsay.** Exhibits 4 and 5 were taken by **Melissa Richards**, a state forensic-lab employee **who did not testify**. Bagley's fair-and-accurate foundation is what **she told him by telephone during trial preparation**, weeks before — he had never seen or discussed those photographs until then. **Admitted over preserved objection under URE 1102 and Utah Const. Art. I §12.**
- **His report omits the direction of the shot** — "I don't see a direction" — and he wrote no supplement. He did **not** use AI to prepare it.
- **Neither he nor anyone he knew of had identified the shooter** by the time he left campus about 9:00 p.m., or the next day while he was there.

### 4.3 The autopsy and State's Exhibit 11 — the medical examiner's report

**This is the most operationally valuable single fact in Day 1 for the library's open questions.**

- **An autopsy was conducted by the Utah Medical Examiner's Office late in the evening of 2025-09-10, possibly into the morning of 09-11, by Dr. Guajardo** (spelled on the record).

- **State's Exhibit 11 is the nine-page report of examination.** Offered 06:05:31; **ADMITTED over defence hearsay and confrontation objection**, under **URE 1102(b)(5)** ("medical and autopsy reports and records") and Utah Const. Art. I §12. **Publication DENIED** — not shown in the courtroom, not viewable by the media camera — on Art. I §28 victim-dignity grounds, both parties effectively agreeing.

⚠ **A correction on the stand, and the court checking the document itself.** Asked the **cause** of death Hull answered "**It was stated as a murder**," and asked the **manner** answered "a gunshot wound to the neck" — **reversing the two and misstating the manner.** The defence objected; **the judge examined page 1 himself** — "**It does not say, Murder**" — and **sustained**. Hull then corrected from the document: **manner of death "Homicide"; immediate cause of death "a gunshot wound of the neck."** The defence's Best Evidence objection was overruled and the exhibit stayed in.

> **Analyst layer — why this matters beyond Day 1.**
> **(a) Q-15 / D-49 now has a located decisive artifact.** D-49 concerns the **fragment count** — a medical examiner's photograph reportedly showing seven fragments where ATF received five. Q-15 names "the medical examiner's report + photograph" as decisive. **That report is Exhibit 11, it is nine pages, its author is named, and it has been in evidence since 2026-07-06.** The library did not know this. It does **not** resolve D-49 — the fragment count is not established by anything read here — but the target is now concrete.
> **(b) A caution about the manner/cause exchange.** It is **not** evidence of anything about the death; it is evidence about **recall under examination**, and the defence used it that way. The court's response — reading the document rather than accepting the witness's characterisation — is the correct handling and is recorded as such. **"Homicide" is a medical examiner's classification of manner, not a legal finding of culpability, and nothing here bears on who caused the death.**

### 4.5 Identification of Robinson, and the doorbell canvass

- **Investigative priority on 09-10 was identifying the person on the roof.** Hundreds of hours of video were available, predominantly UVU's; **Hull personally watched perhaps 10–20 hours that day.**
- **The name arrived from outside the investigation.** On the evening of **the 11th**, investigators "received word from **Washington County** that an individual in that area had **reached out to law enforcement and was wanting to turn themselves in** for the incident at UVU." **The name received was Tyler Robinson.**
- **A Ring doorbell canvass** produced **Ex. 12.2** — a sequence of short doorbell videos of a vehicle at about **00:38 on 2025-09-11** — admitted over objection, with **Ex. 13** (Ms. Noble's 1102) as foundation.
