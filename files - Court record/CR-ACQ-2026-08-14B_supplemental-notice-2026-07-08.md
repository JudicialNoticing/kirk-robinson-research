## 3. What the filing facially shows (recording layer only — fidelity to the claim, not correctness)

- **Caption and parties.** Fourth Judicial District Court, County of Utah; *The State of Utah v. Tyler Robinson*, Case No. 251403576. Counsel block: **Jeffrey A. Neiman** (Neiman Mays Floch & Almeida PLLC) and **Brennan Moss**, Utah Bar #10267 (Parkinson Benson Potter) — "Attorney for Erika Kirk." Signature (p. 3) and certificate (p. 4) are `/s/ Jeffrey A. Neiman` alone.
- **Who files it.** "COMES NOW Erika Kirk, the widow and duly recognized victim representative of Charlie Kirk, together with the parents of Charlie Kirk (collectively, the 'Victim's Family')." It **supplements "the Notice re Media Access filed February 2, 2026"** — a filing this library had never before seen referenced (→ Q-66).
- **The grievance (§I, ¶¶1–2).** The family "sat in the room while evidence was admitted but not presented for their viewing… present in body, yet denied the very thing their presence was meant to secure: their ability to *meaningfully* observe." ¶2: "At a minimum, every exhibit entered into evidence during the preliminary hearing must be visible to every person lawfully present in the courtroom. To receive evidence in a manner shielded from those seated in the courtroom—**as happened today**—is not transparency. And in the absence of transparency, speculation and conspiracy theories related to the tragic assassination of Mr. Kirk will continue to proliferate in the public domain, breeding doubt and distrust in the judicial system."
- **The legal basis asserted (§II, ¶¶3–5).** Utah Const. art. I, § 28(1)(b); *State v. Casey*, 44 P.3d 756, 762 (Utah 2002); Utah Code §§ 77-38-4(1)(b), 77-37-1; *State v. Lopez*, 2020 UT 61, 474 P.3d 949. **None of these authorities has been verified by this project** (the CR-E07 F-17 discipline has not been run on them).
- **Relief requested (§III, ¶6).** (a) that exhibits admitted "during the last three days that were not published to the courtroom" be published "during the proceedings scheduled for July 9, 2026"; (b) that exhibits admitted during the remainder of the hearing "be displayed openly and in real time"; (c) "that no evidence be received by the Court in a manner that conceals it from those lawfully present in the courtroom."
- **Certificate of Delivery (p. 4).** "I certify that I e-filed a copy of the foregoing SUPPLEMENTAL NOTICE, thereby providing notice to the parties in this case, **on July 8, 2026.**" `/s/ Jeffrey A. Neiman`.
