### The evidentiary picture, in brief

The State's case as pleaded rests on a **single aggravating circumstance** — great risk of death to someone other than the victim — plus a victim-targeting enhancement on three counts. Its probable-cause narrative combines surveillance reconstruction, the recovered towel-wrapped bolt-action rifle with engraved cartridges, DNA likelihood ratios, a handwritten note, a text exchange, and the family's account of the surrender. **All of it is recorded here as allegation and adopted nowhere.**

Against that, the record carries genuine unresolved conflicts the library has registered rather than smoothed: the **pleaded distance versus the measured distance** (D-74), and the **charging document's rendering of the note's tense against the note itself** (D-75). Both remain **open**.
