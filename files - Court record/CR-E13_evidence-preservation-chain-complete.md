## §3 · What the operative order actually requires

**¶1 — Set-aside.** The 2025-10-20 order (Dkt. #107) is **SET ASIDE**.

**¶2 — Who and what.** Bound: **the Utah County Attorney's Office, its agents, and any law enforcement agencies involved in the investigation of this case.** Duty: preserve **all evidence in their custody or under their control, involved in the investigation of this case.**
- ⚠️ **No category list** — no mention of biological, DNA, digital, ballistic or documentary evidence. **One undifferentiated class.**
- ⚠️ **No materiality qualifier.** The State's proposed *"all **material** evidence"* **did not survive**.
- 🚨 **It reaches nobody else. NO FEDERAL AGENCY IS NAMED.** 🛠 *(NARROWED 2026-08-16: v1.0 read "named **or described**." **The "named" limb is CONFIRMED** — re-verified by a 23-pattern stem battery **and** by a pattern-independent entity census enumerating every organisation-shaped string in the eight documents; the complete list is the Utah County Attorney's Office, three defence firms, the court, and generic classes. **No law enforcement agency of ANY kind — federal, state or local — is named.** ⚠️ **But the "or described" limb is not testable by any pattern battery and contradicts §7's own correct formulation**: ¶2's class, *"any law enforcement agencies involved in the investigation of this case,"* **IS a description that may or may not encompass a federal agency** — which is exactly why §7 says whether a federal laboratory falls inside it is **not established**. §3 asserted the stronger form; §7 states the weaker and correct one.)*

**¶3 — Release / disposal exit.** The State may release or dispose of an item if it notifies defence counsel of intent **and** counsel does not object **within 14 days** or agrees.

**¶4 — Pre-testing notice — the operative provision for the DNA question.** The State and any involved law enforcement agency **must notify defence counsel prior to any potentially destructive testing and/or examination**, with the same 14-day non-objection/agreement window. And the order **defines the trigger**:

> Testing or examination is potentially destructive **only** when it is likely to **entirely consume** a piece of evidence, or an extract from it, or is likely to **change the character** of a piece of evidence.

**Duration:** ⚠️ **none stated.** No sunset, no retention period, no reference to the Utah evidence-retention schedules both parties briefed.

**F-70 — 🚨 The definition is RESTRICTIVE, not illustrative, and the word doing the work is "only." Confidence: High.** It **narrows** the notice trigger. **Testing that consumes part of a sample but is not likely to entirely consume the piece or the extract, and does not change the item's character, falls outside ¶4 on the order's face.** ⚠️ **This bears directly on this library's DNA work, which turns on consumption thresholds.** No file may describe ¶4 as a general pre-testing notice duty without this qualifier.

**F-71 — The order is silent on what happens if the defence DOES object. Confidence: High.** ¶3 and ¶4 both state only the **non-objection** path. Neither says what follows a timely objection, and neither says which governs testing that is *also* a disposal. **This is a genuine gap on the face of the operative document**, not a gap in this library's reading.

**F-72 — ¶4 is a NEGOTIATED STIPULATED TERM, not a judicial ruling on due process. Confidence: High.** The order's own recital refers to *"the parties' stipulation regarding the wording of an order,"* and the register records the 2025-11-03 stipulated motion. **The court made no finding on the due-process question the State briefed at length.** 🚨 **No file may cite ¶4 as the court's holding on what due process requires.** *(This confirms CR-E09's F-28 from the documents themselves — logged as a confirmation.)*
