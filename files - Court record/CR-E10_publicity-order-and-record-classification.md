## §2 · Recording layer

### 2.1 The publicity order, as quoted by the court clarifying it

Dkt. 242 records that *"The court issued a Protection Order Regarding Pre-Trial and Trial Publicity in this case. (Dkt. 23)."* It quotes the order as prohibiting attorneys associated with the prosecution and defence from *(OCR-dependent quotation)*:

> *"intentionally, knowingly, or recklessly permit[ting] a witness, investigator, consultant, staff member, or other member of the prosecution or defense team to make extrajudicial statements which, if made by the lawyer, would violate Rule 3.6. To that end, lawyers participating in the investigation or litigation of this case shall inform all witnesses, investigators, consultants, staff members, or other members of the prosecution or defense teams about the prohibitions contained in this order."*

**The State moved to clarify** the word *"witness"* as *"vague, ambiguous, and overbroad"* (Dkts. 167, 204), **asserting that there are over 3,000 lay witnesses of the alleged homicide** and that it did not know whether it had to inform every potential witness.

**The court granted the motion and clarified** *(OCR-dependent)*: *"witness" as used in the order applies to all witnesses who are part of the prosecution and defense teams*, including *"any witness, including lay witnesses, whom the prosecution or defense has a good faith belief will be called to testify at a hearing or trial."* It further clarified that the order *"regulates the behavior of attorneys who are associated with the defense team or the Utah County Attorney's Office,"* requiring them to abide by Rule 3.6 and to inform witnesses of the prohibitions — and, decisively:

> ⚠️ *"The publicity order does not regulate the behavior of any person who is not an attorney associated with this case."*

A footnote records that **defence counsel represented they intended to file a motion to amend the publicity order**, which the court would address when filed and briefed. **Whether that motion was filed is not established by anything this project holds.**

### 2.2 Attachment A — the prosecutor's-office media guidance (Dkt. 355, filed 2026-01-30)

The exhibit is an email chain. Its substance:

- On **2025-09-12 at 7:54 a.m.** — two days after the shooting — **Christopher D. Ballard**, identified in the signature block as *General Counsel / Chief of Staff* and *Public Information Officer* of the Utah County Attorney's Office, emailed police chiefs across Utah County agencies, a university and the FBI, copying the County Attorney and two deputies, attaching a letter *"regarding ethical rules that bind prosecutors and, by extension, law enforcement agencies in making statements to the media,"* and asking recipients to *"ensure that your PIO's and other officers strictly adhere to this guidance, especially in making statements regarding the Charlie Kirk shooting at UVU."*
- The attached letter, dated **2025-09-12** and addressed *"To all personnel likely to make media statements regarding the Charlie Kirk case,"* invokes the office's ethical obligation to *"exercise reasonable care to prevent investigators, law enforcement personnel"* and others from making comments with *"a substantial likelihood of materially prejudicing"* the process, cites **Rules 3.6 and 3.8(e)**, and states that *"The point of the rules is ensure the defendant is afforded a fair trial by an impartial jury."* Its stated key limitations include that **statements should be limited to information in the public record** — *"the probable cause statement used in booking and legal papers filed in the case, like the charging document"* — plus that the investigation is ongoing, the next procedural step, the date and place of arrest, and the names of officers and agencies involved.
- The chain was **forwarded internally on 2026-01-21** and filed as an attachment on **2026-01-30**.

### 2.3 The sealed hearing of 2025-10-24 and its records (Dkt. 152)

A **stipulated** motion by the defence, joined by the State, asking the court to order preparation of a transcript from **a sealed hearing held 2025-10-24** and to classify **both the audio recording and the transcript as "private"** under Utah R. Jud. Admin. 4-202.04(3) and 4-202.02(4)(CC). The motion recites that because the hearing was sealed, the records are **presumed sealed** under 4-202.02(3)(l), and argues "private" is the appropriate balance so the parties can use the record in later filings and on appeal — stating that *"request for appellate review during the pendency and/or at the conclusion of the case, is a foregone conclusion."*

### 2.4 The Hull subpoena (Dkt. 291)

The **State** subpoenaed **Sgt. David Hull, State Bureau of Investigations**, to appear **2026-01-16 at 1:00 p.m.** for a hearing type recorded as **"Oral Arguments"** in Courtroom 8B. Issued 2026-01-09 over the signature of Utah County Attorney Jeffrey S. Gray.

## §4 · Findings

**F-31 — The publicity order is docket #23, and Q-71 now has a precise target instead of a description.**
Grade: **High.** CR-E09 F-30 established only that the publicity order was *not* the victim protective order at docket #14. It is **"Protection Order Regarding Pre-Trial and Trial Publicity," docket #23.** Its operative prohibition is now known at one remove, through the court's own later quotation of it. **The order itself remains unheld**, and the quoted passage is the *permitting-others* limb — **the balance of the order, including whatever it says about attorneys' own statements, is unread.** ⚠️ **That limb matters most for Q-65 and is precisely the part not quoted.**

**F-32 — The publicity order binds attorneys, and expressly no one else.**
Grade: **High** as to the clarification. The court stated that the order *"does not regulate the behavior of any person who is not an attorney associated with this case,"* and that its witness-notification requirement is a mechanism for attorneys **to deter** non-attorney prejudicial statements, not a direct constraint on non-attorneys. **"Witness" was narrowed** to team-affiliated witnesses, including lay witnesses either side has a good-faith belief it will call.
**Why the library needs this:** the contempt matter concerns statements by a **prosecutor** — an attorney associated with the Utah County Attorney's Office, squarely inside the order's scope as clarified. **This file states that as a matter of the order's scope only.** It resolves nothing about what was said, whether it violated Rule 3.6, or what the court found.

**F-33 — The State represented that over 3,000 lay witnesses exist.**
Grade: **High** as to the representation; **unverified** as to the number. A scale fact this library did not have, and one that bears on several open items: it explains why the State sought clarification rather than notifying every witness, and it is a useful counterweight to any assumption that the witness pool is small. ⚠️ **It is a party's assertion in a motion, not a finding, and no file may state it as an established count.**

**F-34 — The office's own media-statement guidance, filed as an exhibit in the disqualification litigation, was written and circulated on 2025-09-12 by the attorney who was later held in civil contempt over public statements about this case.**
Grade: **High** as to authorship, date and content — all read directly from the filed exhibit.
⚠️ **This finding is recorded because it is a documentary fact bearing on a public question, and it is bounded severely.** **(a)** The contempt ruling is **not held** by this project; it is known only through reporting (Q-65), so **the finding of contempt itself is T1 via T3 and is not restated here as established.** **(b)** **Nothing in the guidance letter is inconsistent on its face with anything** — the library holds neither the statements complained of nor the ruling assessing them, and **this file draws no inference of hypocrisy, bad faith, or knowledge.** **(c)** The guidance is addressed to **law-enforcement personnel and PIOs**, not primarily to prosecutors, and its stated purpose is to satisfy the office's Rule 3.8(e) duty to prevent *others'* prejudicial statements.
**What it establishes, narrowly and usefully: the office had circulated written internal guidance on exactly this subject, two days after the shooting, and that guidance was in the record of this case by 2026-01-30.** Anyone reconstructing the contempt matter needs that document and that date.

**F-35 — At least one hearing in this case is sealed, and the parties jointly sought to keep its records non-public.**
Grade: **High** as to the motion. The 2025-10-24 hearing was **sealed**, its transcript and audio **presumed sealed** by rule, and **both parties stipulated** that they be classified **private** rather than public — expressly so the parties could use them in later filings and on appeal.
⚠️ **This is a scope limit on every transcript ambition this library has, including Q-36 (the certified transcript).** **Not all proceedings in this case are publicly transcribable, and at least one is not.** The library's five hearing-day transcripts are of public proceedings; **nothing establishes how many other hearings are sealed**, and the docket (CR-ACQ-2026-08-14C) shows a dense run of closure and classification litigation from 2026-01 to 2026-02 that this project has not read.

### Added at v1.1 — 2026-08-14 · four more documents, and Q-75 substantially advanced

**F-36 — SEALING IN THIS CASE IS NOT A ONE-OFF. A SECOND CLOSURE EVENT IS ON THE RECORD, AND IT WAS PARTIAL, WITNESS-SPECIFIC, AND STIPULATED.**
Grade: **High.** The court's **minute order for the evidentiary hearing of 2026-02-03** (Dkt. 413, entered 2026-02-23, signed by Judge Graf through the clerk) records, in the court's own words: *"Ms. Nester addresses court on the next witness and moves the court to close the hearing. Mr. McBride responds and discussion ensues. **Counsel stipulate to close the hearing for the next witness.** The court addresses counsel and **denies in part and grants in part the request**."*
**So the library now has two distinct closure events**: the **wholly sealed** hearing of 2025-10-24 (F-35) and this **partial, witness-specific closure** — sought by the defence, stipulated to by the State, and **granted only in part** by the court. ⚠️ **Closure here is granular: it can attach to a single witness within an otherwise open hearing.** Any future map of what is publicly transcribable must work at the witness level, not the hearing level.

**F-37 — The elected Utah County Attorney testified under oath in this case, examined by defence counsel and cross-examined by his own deputy.**
Grade: **High** as to the fact of it. The same minute order records: *"Mr. Jeff Gray retakes the stand. He is instructed that is under oath. Mr. Novak continues with direct. … Mr. McBride crosses. Witness is excused."* **"Retakes"** establishes this was a continuation — he had testified earlier in the disqualification proceeding.
⚠️ **Recorded as a procedural fact and nothing more.** This project holds neither his testimony nor the ruling on the disqualification motion, and **nothing here bears on whether the motion had merit.** It is logged because a prosecuting authority's head giving sworn evidence in his own office's case is an unusual posture the library had no record of, and because **his testimony is an unheld Tier 1 artifact** on a matter the library tracks only through its downstream effects.

**F-38 — An interlocutory appeal was taken to the Utah Supreme Court and DENIED, and the library had no record of a parallel appellate track.**
Grade: **High.** *Tyler James Robinson v. State of Utah*, **Utah Supreme Court Case No. 20251388-SC**: a **petition for permission to appeal from an interlocutory order, filed 2025-11-17**, with a response filed 2025-12-12, was **denied under Utah R. ⚠️ **The order does not identify which interlocutory order was appealed**, and this project has not established it. **What it establishes is that a second, appellate case number exists in this matter** — a whole docket the library has never looked at, and a route by which orders in this case may have been tested.

**Also recorded (recording layer):** the news media's litigation posture is now visible in the defence's own responses. **Deseret News et al.** sought (a) **notice of any motion to seal, close or limit access** (2025-10-30, Doc. 150) and (b) **limited intervention** (2025-11-24, Dkt. 197) — expressly *"to seek to unseal any such proceeding or record that has been sealed or closed to the public, and to potentially oppose in whole or in part any motion seeking a gag order and/or seek to modify any existing such order."* **The defence opposed intervention**, arguing the requests were *"overly broad and burdensome"* and that *"the only parties to this litigation are, and should remain, Mr. Robinson and the State of Utah."* ⚠️ **A second, earlier media-intervenor motion also exists (Dkt. 164, answered at Dkt. 185) — neither is held.** ⚠️ The defence's filings characterise the matter as *"this death penalty litigation."*
