## 3. THE STIPULATED FINGERPRINT EXCLUSION

Read into the record by Mr. McBride as **language agreed by both parties**:

> The parties stipulate that investigators at the crime scene observed latent fingerprints and a palm print on a glass window near the area where the individual landed when the individual jumped from the rooftop of the Losee Center at UVU. SBI… fingerprint examiner, Elisa Farmer, examined images of these latent fingerprints and the palm print and determined that only three of the latent prints, 11B, 11C and 11D, were of comparable or of poor comparable value. **She excluded Tyler Robinson as the source of any of these three latent prints.**

**The remainder, now read (v1.2) — and it changes the weight considerably.** The stipulation continues:

> Ms. Farmer reviewed other prints on the window and found that they did not have adequate value for comparison. **Subsequent examinations of these three latent prints by the FBI were inconclusive.**

**And the surveillance video answers the question the prints were taken to answer.** On direct, Faumuina testified that the command post's initial footage was "pretty grainy," but once investigators obtained the footage **directly from UVU** they could zoom in — and:

> **McBride:** "…were you able to determine at that time where the suspect touched the window?"
> **Faumuina:** "**We determined that he was not — or he did not touch it.**"

She confirmed on cross that the windows were processed **on September 11**, the day after, and that the purpose was precisely "to rule out whether the suspect had touched that window."

**Analyst layer — v1.0's assessment is materially revised.** v1.0 recorded the exclusion as "a genuine data point in the defendant's favour." **That now looks substantially weaker**, for two independent reasons: the **FBI's own re-examination of the same three prints was inconclusive**, so Farmer's exclusion is not the last word on them; and investigators concluded from better footage that **the person never touched the window at all**, which means nobody's prints there — Robinson's or anyone's — were expected. **An exclusion from prints the suspect is not believed to have left proves very little.**

**Stated fairly in the other direction:** the exclusion is still a real stipulated fact, agreed by both parties, and the "did not touch it" determination is an **investigative conclusion from video review**, not a forensic finding — it rests on the quality of footage this project has not seen. **Confidence: High** that all three things were testified to; **Medium** that the exclusion carries little weight, since that assessment depends on the video determination being sound. **Net: v1.0 over-weighted this in the defendant's favour, and the correction is recorded rather than quietly dropped.**

## 4. THE RIFLE RECOVERY

Shortly before **6:00 PM on 2025-09-10**, Faumuina received a call from the Utah County Sheriff's Office, which was searching the wooded area. They searched it because UVU security footage — received at the on-scene command post — "showed who we believe was the shooter on the rooftop run from the southwest corner all the way to the northeast corner, jump down and then continue" onward.

> "In the bushes we found… all you could really see was just a dark towel there. So pictures were taken of that and then eventually the… towel was removed. And then once we removed the towel from the bushes is when we uncovered that the towel was actually used to wrap the gun in."

**Established (High, as testimony):** a rifle **wrapped in a dark-coloured towel** was recovered from the wooded area on the evening of 2025-09-10; photographs were taken before removal (State's Exhibits 10, 10.1–10.3, admitted); the search was prompted by surveillance footage of a person leaving the Losee roof.

**Firearm mechanics, explained by the witness.** The rifle is **bolt action**: the user must manually work the bolt to load, lock and extract, unlike a semi-automatic that extracts and re-feeds on each trigger pull. **Consequence testified to:** a spent case remains in the chamber unless manually extracted — and **State's Exhibit 19 is "the cartridge case that was recovered from the gun," from the chamber** (04:55:45). ⚠ **Not to be confused with the defence's "Exhibit 19"** — three unadmitted bullet-comparison photographs shown to Karner at 07:33–07:36 (CR-E01 §3.6). The transcript renders both bare. **Always write "State's" or "the defence's" for this number.**

**Chain of custody (High).** The firearm and "the cartridges and cartridge case" were packaged and "originally provided to the **FBI**, then… sent to the **ATF** lab." Faumuina corrected herself on the record to establish that order. Robinson's own fingerprints and DNA were taken at the Utah County Sheriff's Office Jail after transport.

**Cross-file note (CR-E01/CR-ACQ) — this materially limits the `source identification`.** The cartridge case the ATF source-identified was **recovered from inside the rifle's chamber**, and the accompanying cartridges came from the same weapon. Supporting the identity of the items across the two exhibit-numbering systems: the ATF received exactly **one fired case plus three cartridges**, the fired case bore a **Remington** headstamp, and Faumuina confirmed the gun's contents went to the FBI and then to the ATF. **This is a well-supported inference, not a stated fact, and is graded as such (Medium-high).**

> ⚠ **THIS GRADE IS UNDER CHALLENGE — Q-42, human-gated.** The independent pass (CR-AUDIT-2026-07-28 §2.2) found that Faumuina was asked the mapping question **directly** while reading Exhibit 34, and answered it (04:55 numbering aside, day4 **05:39:52–05:40:01**): *"And then, also the Exhibit 2 cartridge case, what is that?"* — *"This would've been the cartridge case that was found in the gun."* — *"And Exhibits 3, 4, and 5 cartridges?"* — *"They were the ones also found in the gun."* On that passage the identity is **sworn testimony, not an inference**. **The grade above is left unchanged pending a human decision**, because raising it would touch **D-48**, which has already moved four times — past LOOP.md §4's third-movement hard stop. **The direction of D-48 is unaffected either way**; only the strength of the caveat attached to it would change.

**The analytical consequence is significant.** A fired case found *inside* a rifle is expected to identify to that rifle; the identification is close to tautological as a link between the weapon and the *shooting*. **On the portion of the record read, no cartridge case was recovered at the perch or anywhere else at the scene** — and the only projectile evidence from the body, the 6A jacket fragment, was **inconclusive**. **Therefore the ATF firearms examination did not link this rifle to the shooting.** What links Robinson to the rifle on this record is **DNA** (§5.1), not ballistics. Anyone citing CR-ACQ's `source identification` finding must carry this limitation — **and D-48 has been re-resolved in consequence** (CR-ACQ §6, v1.3). *(Whether a case was recovered at the perch remains an explicit open question, §6 item 3 — a scoped null from a keyword-sampled read, not a finding of absence.)*

**RESOLVED on full reading — there was never a conflict.** The keyword-sampled extract cut off mid-answer. Faumuina's complete testimony gives **three distinct measurements of one geometry**:

> "…the distance, the perch to the tent was **approximately 415 feet**. If you think about it… **without taking into account the elevation drop, it was actually 410 feet**. So just horizontal straight across. And then **the elevation difference between that is approximately 68 feet**."

So: **415 ft = direct line-of-sight (slope) distance; 410 ft = horizontal distance; 68 ft = vertical drop.** She also described, and counsel put on the record, a downward angle from perch to tent.

**Independent check — the geometry is internally consistent.** √(410² + 68²) = √172,724 ≈ **415.6 ft**. The three figures agree to within rounding. **This is a confirmation, not a conflict**, and it slightly *raises* confidence in the measurements: a witness reciting numbers that satisfy Pythagoras is reciting a real survey, not an approximation.

**Two lessons, both recorded rather than buried.** First, **D-50 should never have been assigned** — it was an artifact of reading a fragment, and the adversarial pass that created it was itself working from the truncated extract. Second, and more useful: **the pass was still right to flag it.** Given only the fragment, refusing to silently pick 410 over 415 was correct; the error was in the underlying sampling, not in the skepticism. **The register entry is retained, resolved, rather than deleted** — a resolved non-discrepancy is a cheaper record than a number nobody can later re-check.
