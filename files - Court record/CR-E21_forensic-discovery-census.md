**Tier 1** — sworn testimony and primary court filings/documents · **Tier 2** — named official statements · **Tier 3** — named journalistic reporting/analysis · **Tier 4** — anonymous or low-verification. **"T1 via T3"** marks primary-document content known only through reporting.

## §4 · 🚨 WHAT THE DEFENCE SAID IT HAD NOT RECEIVED — and where it lands

Part One runs to **eighteen numbered categories**. **Seventeen carry the parenthetical *"We have not been provided with any of this material"*** or a near-identical formula. The exceptions are narrow and are recorded exactly:

- **The FBI's DNA SOP testing protocols** — received. ⚠ **"We have not been provided the ATF DNA SOP testing protocols."**
- **The CV of Amanda N. Bakker** — received, and it is the **only** item received under category 18 (laboratory personnel).
- **"Basic chain of custody reports"** — received, *"that indicate nothing about storage conditions, etc."*

**`CR-E21 F-3` — As at 2026-03-25 the defence had NOT been provided the consumption and disposition records, and those are exactly the artifacts `DNA-E01` §6.4 names as decisive. Confidence: High as to the letter's assertion.**

Request **5** asks for chain-of-custody documents for every item subjected to DNA testing, *"through to the current disposition of that item,"* expressly including:

> *"documentation which indicates where and how the materials were stored (temperature and type of container), **the amount of evidence material which was consumed in testing, the amount of material which remains**, and where and how the remaining evidence is stored."*

**Answer: *"We have not been provided with any of this material, except for basic chain of custody reports that indicate nothing about storage conditions."***

> 🚨 **WHY THIS MATTERS.** **`DNA-E01` §6 is the consumption-and-irreproducibility question** — six of eleven ratio-bearing ATF items entirely consumed, and Bakker's unrebutted, untested claim that the FBI *"returned the remaining extract… I believe on all of the items."* **§6.4 names "the FBI case file's consumption records" and "evidence of what was actually returned to the field and where it is now" as what would settle it.**
> **This letter establishes that as at 2026-03-25 — four months before the preliminary hearing — the DEFENCE did not have those records either.**
> ⚠ **WHAT THIS DOES AND DOES NOT ESTABLISH.** It does **not** establish that the records do not exist, that they were withheld improperly, or that anything was destroyed. **Discovery in a capital-eligible case is staged and ongoing, and the letter says so.** What it establishes is that **the hearing's consumption testimony was given against a production record in which the underlying disposition documentation had not been produced to the defence.** **No inference of misconduct is drawn or licensed.**

**`CR-E21 F-4` — Probabilistic genotyping output had not been produced. Confidence: High as to the letter's assertion.** Request **7** seeks *"all computer folders and data files used or created in the course of probabilistic genotyping analysis… all timestamped folders outputted by a probabilistic genotyping system"* in native format. **Not provided.** Request **9** (STRmix data) — **not provided.** ⚠ **`DNA-E01` §3 and §4 rest substantially on STRmix behaviour described by witnesses; the underlying output was not in defence hands when this letter was written.**

## §5 · The two expert statements — and one of them is sworn

**`CR-E21 F-5` — A defence DNA expert stated under penalty of perjury that TWO FEDERAL AGENCIES are testing DNA in this case and that she needs ~six months after complete production. Confidence: High (Tier 1 sworn).**

**Addendum 2 — Declaration of Elizabeth A. Johnson, Ph.D.**, executed **2026-03-27**, Ventura County, California, **under penalty of perjury**. Ph.D. immunology (Medical University of South Carolina, 1987); established and directed the DNA laboratory at the Harris County Medical Examiner's Office from 1991; in private forensic-biology practice since 2003. She declares:

> *"**There are two federal agencies testing DNA evidence in this case.** I have received a large amount of discovery to date, and I expect to receive more discovery for some time to come."* … *"it is my professional opinion that in order to perform a complete and reliable review I will need **approximately six months after all discovery material has been produced**."* … she agrees the requested material *"is necessary for my complete independent review of **the FBI's and the ATF's DNA laboratory work** in this case."*

⚠ **This is the first sworn Tier 1 statement in this library that the DNA work is split across two federal laboratories** — a fact the library had assembled from testimony and `DNA-E01 F-8`. **It confirms the division independently, from the defence side, in a sworn document.**

**Exhibit G — Dan E. Krane, Ph.D.**, CEO, Forensic Bioinformatics, letter of **2026-03-27**. ⚠ **Not sworn; a consultant's availability letter.** He states he understands that *"**a larger than usual number of samples were tested** in Mr. Robinson's case by **at least two different laboratories** and that **much of the testing has involved the use of probabilistic genotyping** to assist with the interpretation of results that are **not suitable for conventional analyses**."*

⚠ **RECORDING LAYER, AND THE HEDGE IS THE POINT: "I understand that…" is Krane relaying his instructions from counsel, not an independent finding.** He had not yet reviewed the case. ⚠ **It may not be cited as an expert opinion on the evidence.** What it does supply is a professional estimate of review time — three to four weeks for an initial review in circumstances like these, plus up to a further month if internal validation data must be litigated.
