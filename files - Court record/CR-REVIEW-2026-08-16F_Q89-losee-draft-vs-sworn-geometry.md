**U-2 — *"the only figures in the record are Faumuina's, on Day 4"* is false as to the case record.** The **Day 1** half is properly scoped and survives; the trailing clause is not scoped, and **four more sworn figures had been on the public docket since 2026-01-05 — six months before the preliminary hearing.** *A file that promises stated residual scope cannot carry an unscoped "only."*

**U-3 — §11 Finding 5 publishes its own falsification condition, and it has already been met.** The *"what would change it"* cell names *"a dated campus map"* and *"any measured sight-line study."* **Both now exist in this library's own holdings** — the re-enactment, and a **marked isometric campus rendering** in the same filing. **A finding whose stated trigger has been satisfied inside the same repository is the worst available outcome.**

**U-4 — the 85 ft will be misread as perch geometry unless pre-empted.** The four figures are Kirk↔bag (**85 ft**, measured 2025-09-30), bag→visibility point (**75 ft**), Kirk→loss-of-view point (**8 ft**), Sorensen roof edge→lamp post (**18 ft**). 🚨 **NOT ONE is perch→tent.** They do **not** fill §4.3's provenance gap on 415/410/68 and must never be presented as doing so — and **85 ft is a ground distance inside the amphitheatre, not a Losee-roof distance.**

**U-5 — the four figures are NOT internally checkable, and the draft's own Pythagoras precedent invites a false check.** 85 − 75 = 10 against a separately measured 8. 🚨 **This is not a validity check and must not be published as one.** The 75-ft walk ran along *"the center of the courtyard on the top tier"*; Kirk was *"at the bottom of the amphitheater."* **Different paths, different tiers, unstated elevations.** Where §4.3 can say the three Day 4 figures form one internally consistent survey, **the honest statement here is the opposite** — the near-miss is coincidence-shaped, not corroboration.

## §3 · 🚨 Three further hazards in the evidence itself

**U-6 — THE RE-ENACTMENT IS DECEMBER; THE EVENT IS SEPTEMBER.** Read first-hand from the reproduced photograph: **the deciduous trees are bare**, and the courtyard is **empty — no tent, no stage, no chairs, no crowd**. ⚠️ **The far boundary of the 18-ft window is *"a lamp post next to a tree."*** **A leafless December tree is not a September tree**, and ~3,000 attendees plus event infrastructure are absent from the re-enactment entirely. **The geometry describes 2025-12-18. Its transposition to 2025-09-10 is a connection, not an anchor.**

**U-7 — the 18-ft figure has an unexplained far boundary, and the affidavit never measures the other side.** The affidavit says the Sorensen roof edge *"inhibited the visibility"* but **never says the tree or lamp post obstructed anything**; and **¶8 measures only ONE boundary near Kirk (8 ft, to his left)**, never the right-hand one. **So the affidavit nowhere establishes the width of the visible window at Kirk's position, and never relates the 8 ft and the 18 ft to each other.**

**U-8 — there is NO second sworn declarant.** The affidavit records that a second officer took the rooftop position and that *"I confirmed with him the locations of visibility."* 🚨 **That is the affiant's own account of a confirmation. The second officer swore nothing; no affidavit from him is in the filing.** **Single-declarant evidence, and it must be graded as such.** ⚠️ **CR-E11 §6.1's original wording could be read otherwise and has been narrowed (v1.2).**

**U-9 — the affidavit commits, under oath, the exact conflation the draft's framework warns about.** ¶3 states as fact: *"I went to the rooftop **where Mr. Robinson was positioned when Mr. Kirk was shot**, the southwest corner of the Losee Center."* ⚠️ **A State investigator adopting the perch identification as established, in a filing litigating an unrelated procedural question.** **Recorded as an attributed sworn assertion and adopted by nobody.** It is simultaneously powerful support for the draft's central thesis and **the most dangerous sentence in the file to quote loosely.**

**Not caused by the new evidence. Found by a search the draft never ran, and verified directly against the transcript by the orchestrator.**

⚠️ **Three cautions that must travel with it.** (a) **It is a LIVE round, not a fired projectile** — no file may present it otherwise. 🚨 **But it is a sight-line determination BY INVESTIGATORS, IN THE HEARING RECORD — a negative one, from a different roof — and its absence makes §5's "lay eyeball estimate" framing understate the record twice over.** This is the **completeness-pass failure mode** the library has already recorded: *verifying what a file says cannot find what it never said.*
