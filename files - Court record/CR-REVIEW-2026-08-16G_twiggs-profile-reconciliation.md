## §2 · 🚨 C3 — the single most important correction, and it is applied

**§5.1 read:** *"Mixed male DNA from **two individuals — Robinson and Twiggs — was found on both**"* the towel and the screwdriver.

**The record shows the opposite methodologically.** The FBI examiner testified:

- **The only likelihood ratio ever calculated on Mr. Twiggs alone was `1`** — and on her own verbal scale, an LR of 1 *"would fall under an **uninformative**."*
- *"I **didn't utilize this calculation** to determine that Mr. Twiggs was a possible contributor … I **assumed** he was a contributor."*
- *"Based on the STRmix run, it was **uninformative**."*

🚨 **He was an ASSUMED input to the statistical model, not a statistically supported presence.** ⚠️ **And the deeper point: both headline statistics carry him in BOTH of their competing propositions** — so the statistic is not merely weak as to him, **it never tests his presence at all.** A number that appears on both sides of a ratio cannot discriminate.

✅ **APPLIED to the profile 2026-08-16 (→ v1.2).** The superseded sentence is struck in place, not deleted, with the testimony quoted.
