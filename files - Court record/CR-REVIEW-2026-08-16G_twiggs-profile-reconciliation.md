## §2 · 🚨 C3 — the single most important correction, and it is applied

**§5.1 read:** *"Mixed male DNA from **two individuals — Robinson and Twiggs — was found on both**"* the towel and the screwdriver.

**The record shows the opposite methodologically.** The FBI examiner testified:

- **The only likelihood ratio ever calculated on Mr. Twiggs alone was `1`** — and on her own verbal scale, an LR of 1 *"would fall under an **uninformative**."*
- *"I **didn't utilize this calculation** to determine that Mr. Twiggs was a possible contributor … I **assumed** he was a contributor."*
- *"Based on the STRmix run, it was **uninformative**."*

🚨 **He was an ASSUMED input to the statistical model, not a statistically supported presence.** ⚠️ **And the deeper point: both headline statistics carry him in BOTH of their competing propositions** — so the statistic is not merely weak as to him, **it never tests his presence at all.** A number that appears on both sides of a ratio cannot discriminate.

✅ **APPLIED to the profile 2026-08-16 (→ v1.2).** The superseded sentence is struck in place, not deleted, with the testimony quoted.

## §3 · 🚨 C1 — a quotation attributed to the wrong speaker, and it is the private individual's own line

**The Information renders this as a two-person exchange.** The second clause is **Twiggs' reply**, not Robinson's instruction. The defence's 2026-08-11 brief quotes **only Robinson's side**, which is how the two halves came to be read as one utterance.

🚨 **This put a private individual's words inside a list captioned as a capital defendant's instructions** — wrong in both directions at once: it removes a line from the person who said it, and it adds a line to the person facing the charges.

✅ **APPLIED 2026-08-16.** Verified directly against the Information and the brief before editing.

## §5 · ✅ Q-50's premise CONFIRMED at Tier 1 — in three tiers, not one

**Q-50 asked whether the DNA-bearing items had innocent household provenance.** The answer is **item-specific**, and the three items are *not* alike:

| item | provenance in the record | tier of the answer |
|---|---|---|
| **Dremel** | **inside the shared household** | innocent provenance strongest |
| **Towel** | 🚨 *"found in a bush out at the university… **outside of a home**"* — **conceded by the defence examiner on cross** | innocent provenance **fails** |
| **Screwdriver** | *"**Item 8, Screwdriver from perch**"* — **no household provenance offered at all** | innocent provenance **absent** |

⚠️ **So the "shared household explains the mixture" reasoning holds for one item and collapses for the other two.** A single global answer to Q-50 would have been wrong in both directions.

**✅ And a third person's DNA is on the rifle.** Item **1.4** — Robinson's father — with **the same "associated with the premise" explanation** offered. ⚠️ **This cuts toward the profile's caution, not against it:** the elimination-standard logic that swept Twiggs in swept in a third person nobody alleges was present.
