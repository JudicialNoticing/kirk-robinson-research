# Tier 1 Source Acquisition Roadmap — Forensic Evidence for the Losee Building Shot
## State of Utah v. Tyler James Robinson — Case No. 251403576, Fourth District Court (Provo), Judge Tony Graf

**Purpose:** Inventory of all identified Tier 1 sources (sworn testimony and primary court filings) that are accessible now or could become accessible if specified steps are completed. Organized by acquisition difficulty. Prepared July 13, 2026.

**Definitions reminder (Tier 1):** Sworn testimony and primary court filings — the document or record itself, not reporting about it.

---

## A. Accessible Now — Retrieval Only

**A1. DocumentCloud filings collection.** A maintained public collection of official court filings, docket records, and public documents from the case (DocumentCloud Project 224251, "Charlie Kirk Murder Case and Tyler Robinson") [1]. Free. **Step:** browse/download; verify each document against the docket number stamp before citing.

**A2. Charging document / information (filed Sept. 16, 2025)** with the probable cause summary. Publicly released by the Utah County Attorney; mirrored on DocumentCloud and outlet-hosted PDFs. **Step:** retrieve; prefer the court-stamped version over outlet re-typesets.

**A3. Unsealed search warrant affidavits (unsealed ~April 2026).** Source of the fingerprint/palm-print details near the rooftop's northeast corner [2]. Utah posts unsealed search warrants through the state courts' public warrant repository. **Step:** search the Utah courts warrant database by case/agency/date range; cross-check against East Idaho News' hosted copies.

**A4. ATF firearms examination report (dated Sept. 17, 2025).** Made public as an attachment to the defense's April 2026 filing [3]. Resolves the exact "identified" vs. "consistent with" vs. "inconclusive" wording per item — one of the open questions in the evidence file. **Step:** pull the filing + attachment from DocumentCloud or the docket.

**A5. Previously released official transcripts.** At least one court-produced (redacted) hearing transcript from a Dec. 2025 proceeding is already public as a PDF [4]. **Step:** retrieve; catalog which hearings already have official transcripts released before ordering new ones.

**A6. Third-party verbatim transcripts of the July 2026 preliminary hearing.** Rev has published day-by-day transcripts of the livestreamed proceedings (Day 4 confirmed extant) [5]. **Caveat:** these are private transcriptions of the video feed, not certified court transcripts — treat as Tier 1-adjacent; verify critical passages against the official transcript (B1) or the archived video before citation-locking. **Step:** collect all published days; note gaps where the feed was cut per Judge Graf's shielding orders.

**A7. Archived pool video / livestream recordings of testimony.** Raw footage of sworn testimony (KSL, KUTV, Court TV, and other pool carriers) is Tier 1 content delivered through a secondary carrier. **Step:** archive the full-session videos (not clipped packages); log timestamps for key exchanges (e.g., Hull's no-casings cross-examination).

## B. Obtainable Now — Procedural Steps Required

**B1. Official certified transcripts of the July 6–10, 2026 preliminary hearing.** The gold-standard Tier 1 record; resolves wording disputes third-party transcripts can't. **Steps:** (1) submit a transcript request to the Fourth District Court transcript coordinator for case 251403576, specifying hearing dates; (2) pay per-page fees (expedited rates higher); (3) note that portions subject to sealing orders will be redacted. Utah district courts are courts of record; hearings are recorded either by reporters or electronically [6].

**B2. Electronic audio recordings of hearings.** Copies of electronically recorded hearings are available from the individual court [6] — often faster and cheaper than certified transcripts. **Steps:** contact the Fourth District (Provo) clerk; request the recording for specified dates; pay the media fee. Subject to the same access orders as B1.

**B3. Full docket and e-filed documents via XChange.** XChange is Utah's PACER-equivalent repository of district court dockets and documents; full text of public e-filed documents is increasingly available [7]. **Steps (choose one):** (1) remote subscription through the Utah Administrative Office of the Courts (post-HB 249, non-subscription fee access is also permitted [8]); (2) free public XChange terminals at most Utah district courthouses and the University of Utah law library [7] — impractical from New York without a proxy; (3) targeted records request to the clerk for specific filings identified from the docket.

**B4. Individual filings not yet mirrored publicly.** Includes the defense's standing hearsay objection, the Miranda-related filing with the timestamp conflict, motions in limine on the Twiggs interviews, and the victim-representative filings on exhibit publication. Judge Graf denied a defense motion to seal several filings in March 2026, so the default posture is public [9]. **Steps:** identify by docket entry via B3, then request/download.

**B5. Admitted preliminary hearing exhibits (photos, video compilations, lab reports).** The Kirk family's attorney has formally pushed for public release of admitted evidence [10], and exhibit-publication briefing occurred mid-hearing [5]. **Steps:** (1) monitor Graf's rulings on exhibit release; (2) submit a records request to the clerk for exhibits ruled public; (3) track the media coalition's filings, since their wins define what becomes retrievable. Highest-value targets for the open questions: the FBI DNA reports (item-level likelihood ratios and contributor splits) and ATF worksheets (casing language; FBI/ATF division of labor).

## C. Contingent — Requires Future Events or Rulings

**C1. Probable cause briefs (both parties).** Due ahead of the September 1, 2026 oral arguments. Becomes retrievable via B3/B4 upon filing.

**C2. September 1 oral argument record + Judge Graf's written probable cause ruling.** The ruling will be the single most authoritative synthesis of what the court found the evidence to show. Retrievable via B1–B3 once issued.

**C3. Twiggs' two recorded law-enforcement interviews.** Contingent on the admissibility ruling; if admitted and not sealed, request as exhibits (B5 path).

**C4. Robinson's post-surrender interview video.** Subject of the family's release request [10]; contingent on a release order.

**C5. Autopsy / Medical Examiner report.** Admitted at the prelim over hearsay objection. Utah law restricts ME reports outside next-of-kin, but its status as an admitted exhibit creates a possible release path if Graf rules exhibits public. Would resolve bullet-fragment recovery details underlying the ballistics questions.

**C6. Trial record (if bound over):** witness-by-witness certified testimony, admitted exhibits under trial rules, and jury instructions.

**C7. Federal agency records (FBI, ATF) via FOIA.** Requests now will draw Exemption 7(A) denials (pending proceedings). Viable post-adjudication; file early anyway to establish queue position, expect multi-year timelines.

**C8. State agency records via GRAMA** (DPS/SBI investigative file, UVU PD bodycam and dispatch audio, Utah County Sheriff booking records — the booking-time conflict in the surrender timeline memo lives here). Expect protected-records denials while the prosecution is pending; denials are appealable to the State Records Committee, but the realistic path is post-case. **Step now:** draft the request list so it's ready to file at disposition.
