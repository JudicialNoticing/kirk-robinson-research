## 1. The two mixed-DNA items: towel and screwdriver (FBI Laboratory)

**Analyst:** Amanda Bakker, forensic examiner, FBI Laboratory, Quantico, VA (10+ years). **[T1 via T3]**
**Report author:** Bakker. **Report read into the record by:** Sgt. Jennifer Faumuina, Utah State Bureau of Investigation — *not* by Bakker. (See §5 on the hearsay mechanism; corrected in v2.)
Sources: https://www.kuer.org/news/2026-07-07/charlie-kirk-tyler-robinson-preliminary-hearing-day-2 ; https://thepostmillennial.com/never-before-seen-footage-shown-during-day-2-of-preliminary-hearing-reveals-tyler-robinsons-movements-through-uvu-campus-dna-links-him-to-crime-scene

### Item identifications
- **Item 7(1) = the towel** — dark-colored, recovered wrapped around the rifle in a wooded area near campus.
- **Item 8(1) = the screwdriver** — recovered from the Losee Center rooftop (alleged firing position).

### Likelihood ratios read into the record **[T1 via T3]**
Both items interpreted as originating from **two individuals, one of whom is Twiggs**; the reported statistic conditions on Twiggs and asks whether the *second* contributor is Robinson vs. a random unrelated person:

- **Towel, item 7(1): ~1.7 octillion (1.7 × 10²⁷)** times more likely if the contributors are Twiggs + T. Robinson than Twiggs + an unknown, unrelated person.
- **Screwdriver, item 8(1): ~30 quintillion (3 × 10¹⁹)** times more likely, same hypothesis pair.

Source (verbatim of the two report sections read aloud): https://thepostmillennial.com/never-before-seen-footage-shown-during-day-2-of-preliminary-hearing-reveals-tyler-robinsons-movements-through-uvu-campus-dna-links-him-to-crime-scene ; screwdriver figure independently corroborated: https://www.kuer.org/news/2026-07-07/charlie-kirk-tyler-robinson-preliminary-hearing-day-2

> **Interpretation guardrail:** This is a conditional likelihood ratio — a statement about the probability of the *evidence given competing hypotheses*, not "the probability the DNA is Robinson's." Restating it as "X times more likely to belong to Robinson than anyone else" is a transposed conditional and is inaccurate (error corrected in v2).

### Contributor split (which person, how much) **[T1 via T3]**
Robinson is the **major** contributor and Twiggs the **minor** contributor on both items.
- **Towel:** **95% Robinson / 5% Twiggs.** Now sourced to Bakker's own words (video clip of testimony): she stated Twiggs' profile aligned with the 5% contributor and Robinson's aligned with the 95%. This is the strongest-sourced version and definitively refutes the OANN inversion (see §7, D-1).
  Source: https://conservativebrief.com/dna-emerges-102657/ ; corroboration of direction: https://www.deseret.com/politics/2026/07/10/tyler-robinson-preliminary-hearing-charlie-kirk-assassination-utah-court/ ; https://www.boredpanda.com/fbi-expert-dna-match-tyler-robinson-trial/
- **Screwdriver:** **89% Robinson / 11% Twiggs** — sourced only to Deseret's summary (no verbatim quote located); confidence moderate.
  Source: https://www.deseret.com/politics/2026/07/10/tyler-robinson-preliminary-hearing-charlie-kirk-assassination-utah-court/

### Contributor-count revision (defense theme) **[T1 via T3]**
Initial interpretation indicated **up to three** contributors on the towel; later revised to **two**. Accounts of *why* differ (see §7, D-7): after obtaining Twiggs' elimination sample / after additional testing (prosecution-aligned framing) vs. a "subjective" change (defense framing by Burt).
Sources: https://townhall.com/tipsheet/jeff-charles/2026/07/08/tyler-robinson-hearing-update-n2678962 ; https://thepostmillennial.com/never-before-seen-footage-shown-during-day-2-of-preliminary-hearing-reveals-tyler-robinsons-movements-through-uvu-campus-dna-links-him-to-crime-scene ; https://www.ksl.com/article/51594946/tyler-robinsons-preliminary-hearing-day-2

### Bakker's verbal conclusion and conceded limits **[T1 via T3]**
- FBI verbal conclusion: **"very strong support"** that the towel and screwdriver DNA originated from Robinson and Twiggs (top-tier FBI qualifier). Source: https://www.cnn.com/2026/07/11/us/charlie-kirk-tyler-robinson-hearing-takeaways
- Report used **"possible contributor"** language, not "match" / "absolute identification." Per Bakker, that hedged language is **mandatory U.S. Department of Justice convention**, not a case-specific concession. Source: https://www.the-journal.com/articles/takeaways-from-a-weeklong-preliminary-hearing-in-the-charlie-kirk-murder-case/

Source: https://www.stgeorgeutah.com/news/defense-turns-tables-on-prosecution-in-2nd-day-of-tyler-robinson-hearing/article_51a97b54-dad1-403f-9e66-a50abba22ac6.html

## 2. Rifle and ammunition (ATF Laboratory)

**Analyst:** Caitlin Oliver, ATF — reported both as a **forensic biologist** (discipline) and as a **DNA section chief** (role); both are defensible (see §7, D-3). **[T1 via T3]**
Sources: https://www.the-journal.com/articles/takeaways-from-a-weeklong-preliminary-hearing-in-the-charlie-kirk-murder-case/ ; https://www.cnn.com/2026/07/11/us/charlie-kirk-tyler-robinson-hearing-takeaways

### The weapon
Mauser Model 98 bolt-action rifle, retrofitted/chambered for **.30-06**, recovered wrapped in the towel in a wooded area near campus; consistent with the caliber of bullet fragments from Kirk's autopsy. Contained **four cartridges: one spent casing + three unfired rounds, each engraved with messages** (e.g., a casing reading "Catch!"; a casing engraved "TEST SHOT").
Sources: https://www.foxnews.com/us/tyler-robinson-lance-twiggs-dna-allegedly-found-key-evidence-charlie-kirks-assassination ; https://www.cnn.com/2026/07/11/us/charlie-kirk-tyler-robinson-hearing-takeaways

### DNA statistic (ATF) **[T1 via T3]**
DNA at multiple rifle/ammunition locations supports **Robinson + one unrelated person** over **two unknown, unrelated persons**, at a likelihood ratio of **at least 1 trillion (10¹²)**.
Sources: https://www.usnews.com/news/best-states/utah/articles/2026-07-10/the-latest-defense-lawyers-question-reliability-of-evidence-in-killing-of-charlie-kirk ; https://www.kuer.org/news/2026-07-10/charlie-kirk-tyler-robinson-preliminary-hearing-day-5

> **Two guardrails on the "1 trillion" figure (both critical, corrected in v2):**
> 1. **It is a reporting CEILING, not a measured value.** Per Oliver on cross: the lab caps its reported LR at 1 trillion and would not report a number higher. The true LR could be far greater. Source: https://www.kuer.org/news/2026-07-10/charlie-kirk-tyler-robinson-preliminary-hearing-day-5
> 2. **The ATF hypothesis pair differs from the FBI's** — Twiggs is *not* in the ATF comparison. Therefore the ATF "1 trillion" and the FBI "30 quintillion / 1.7 octillion" are **NOT ordinally comparable.** Ranking them against each other is a category error (invalid comparison; corrected in v2).

### DNA locations on the rifle (Oliver) **[T1 via T3]**
Robinson's DNA reported at: stock and grips, butt plate, trigger and trigger guard, bolt, fore-end, barrel, optical accessory, protected underside of the receiver, one .30-06 cartridge case, and several .30-06 cartridges. Prosecutors separately state DNA consistent with Robinson was on the firearm, the towel, and **three of the four cartridges** inside the rifle. (Wording variance on cartridge count — see §7, D-8-b.)
Sources: https://www.deseret.com/politics/2026/07/10/tyler-robinson-preliminary-hearing-charlie-kirk-assassination-utah-court/ ; https://www.ksl.com/article/news/utah/police-and-courts/judge-unseals-atf-report-in-charlie-kirk-assassination-case/51484705

### ATF's five-tier conclusion scale (Oliver) **[T1 via T3]**
exclusion → limited support for exclusion → uninformative → limited support for inclusion → support for inclusion.
Source: https://www.kuer.org/news/2026-07-10/charlie-kirk-tyler-robinson-preliminary-hearing-day-5

> **Restatement guardrail (Oliver, on cross):** She agreed one could not accurately headline "Mr. Robinson's DNA found on this item" — the finding is statistical support for inclusion, not attribution to a person. Source: https://www.usnews.com/news/best-states/utah/articles/2026-07-10/the-latest-defense-lawyers-question-reliability-of-evidence-in-killing-of-charlie-kirk

## 5. Procedural posture of the forensic testimony (important structural fact)

Corrected in v2 — the widely-used press shorthand ("defense cross-examined the analysts") inverts what happened:

1. **The State proved its forensics through hearsay.** Sgt. Faumuina (a case agent) **read the FBI report's likelihood-ratio sections into the record.** Utah permits reliable hearsay at preliminary hearings, so the State did not need to call the analysts.
2. **The defense CALLED all three forensic experts** as its own witnesses — Bakker (FBI/DNA), Oliver (ATF/DNA), Karner (ATF/firearms-toolmarks) — to attack methodology and highlight limits.
3. **The prosecution then CROSS-EXAMINED the defense's own witnesses**, eliciting some of the most incriminating detail (e.g., Oliver on rifle-location DNA supporting Robinson as the source).
The Bakker sequence specifically: defense direct → prosecution cross → defense redirect (PCAST reliability report).
Sources: https://www.cnn.com/2026/07/11/us/charlie-kirk-tyler-robinson-hearing-takeaways ; https://www.abc4.com/news/charlie-kirk-shooting/tyler-robinson-hearing-resumes-new-law-enforcement-video-expected/ ; https://thefederalist.com/2026/07/10/heres-what-you-need-to-know-about-the-charlie-kirk-assassination-trial-day-5/
