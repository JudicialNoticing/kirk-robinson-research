## 1. Question Under Investigation

A photograph circulated widely in September 2025 — including by the New York Post — depicting a scoped Mauser Model 98-pattern bolt-action rifle lying in an open box, presented as the weapon recovered in the Kirk assassination investigation. The question: was this photo of the actual recovered rifle, or a stock/illustrative image?

**Image-content note (precision):** The photograph itself was not independently examined in the sessions producing this file. The "cardboard box" descriptor derives from commentator descriptions of the matching July 2026 court exhibits; the "on grass" ground-surface detail derives from researcher recollection of the September 2025 image. Both are unverified pending direct examination of an archived copy.

**Finding (as of July 15, 2026):** The weight of evidence indicates the photo depicted the **actual recovered rifle**, photographed during evidence collection at the wooded-area crime scene near UVU. Sworn testimony establishes that investigators photographed the scene and the firearm when it was discovered; law enforcement photos introduced as exhibits at the July 2026 preliminary hearing show the recovered rifle in what commentators identify as the same box seen in the September 2025 image. The "stock photo" claim traces to unsourced social media commentary and is unsupported in the retrieved record. The decisive link between the September 2025 image and the court exhibits is a commentator-level visual match, not sworn authentication (see §3.5, §4.1). Residual caveats in §5.

## 3. Evidence Items

### 3.1 The NY Post publication (Tier 3)
On September 11, 2025, the New York Post released an image it presented as the rifle believed used in the assassination. Contemporaneous aggregation confirms the framing as a purported photo of the actual weapon.
- Contemporaneous aggregation: https://x.com/MDNnewss/status/1966205064676258000
- Same-day Post coverage context (shooter POV / sniper's nest photos): https://www.latintimes.com/hunter-rifle-cost-under-1000-alleged-weapon-used-kill-charlie-kirk-utah-589458

**Access-path caveat:** The original NY Post article — including its exact photo caption and credit language — was **not retrieved directly** in either research session (a retrieval attempt on July 15, 2026 did not surface the original article). Whether the Post credited the image as "obtained" from sources is therefore unverified; the framing is established via secondary references. Direct retrieval (archive.org / archive.today) remains an open acquisition item.

### 3.2 The stock-photo claim (Tier 4)
Ian Carroll and other online commentators claimed the NY Post image was a stock photo rather than the actual rifle. No sourced basis for the claim (e.g., an identified matching stock listing) appears in the retrieved record; it circulated as assertion. The start date of the claim's circulation is not established in the retrieved record.
- Referenced and rebutted in: https://hotair.com/john-s-2/2026/07/10/day-five-of-tyler-robinsons-preliminary-hearing-n3816817

### 3.3 Genuine stock imagery in parallel coverage (Tier 3 — verifiable credit)
NBC News's September 20, 2025 feature on the rifle used a properly credited stock photograph of a generic Mauser 98 (credit: Wirestock, Inc. / Alamy) as illustrative art. This is a **different image** from the NY Post's.
- https://www.nbcnews.com/news/us-news/kirk-assassins-alleged-gun-was-powerful-vintage-hard-trace-rcna231334

**Analytical note (inference, explicitly labeled):** The verified presence of credited stock Mauser images in mainstream coverage plausibly contributed to conflation — a real stock Mauser photo *was* circulating, but as illustration, not as the Post's purported evidence photo. This is the author's inference, not testified fact or reported conclusion.

### 4.1 Reasoning structure (logic audit)

- **P1 (T1 via T3):** Sworn testimony establishes law enforcement photographed the rifle and scene at discovery — the photo class exists.
- **P2 (T1 content via T3):** Court exhibits displayed July 9, 2026 include law enforcement photos of the recovered rifle in the woods, in a towel, unwrapped, and (per commentator description) in a box.
- **P3 (T3/T4 inference):** Commentators assert visual identity between the September 2025 NY Post image and the P2 exhibit photos (same rifle, same box).
- **C:** The NY Post image was very likely an authentic evidence-collection photo of the recovered rifle.

C depends on P3. P1 and P2 make P3 plausible (a matching photo class demonstrably exists) but do not independently establish it. Alternative hypotheses considered: (a) the NY Post image is stock and coincidentally resembles the exhibits — implausible given the claimed setting/box specificity, but the rebuttal runs through P3; (b) the image is authentic but depicts a different firearm — disfavored by the consistency of the depicted configuration (scoped Mauser 98-pattern bolt-action) with sworn descriptions; (c) the court exhibits derive from the leaked image rather than vice versa — no support, and inconsistent with P1. Conclusion is therefore stated as "substantially corroborated pending exhibit-level comparison," not as established fact.

## 5. Unresolved Items

1. **Original NY Post caption/credit wording** — not directly retrieved (retrieval attempted and unsuccessful July 15, 2026); needed to establish exactly how the Post sourced and framed the image. Acquisition path: archive.org / archive.today capture of the Sept. 11–12, 2025 article.
2. **No direct sworn authentication of the NY Post photo** — hearing exhibits corroborate by visual match (commentator-level); no testimony retrieved that addresses the leaked September 2025 image specifically or its chain of custody to the Post.
3. **Provenance of the leak** — no official has confirmed who provided the image to the Post; presumptively a law-enforcement evidence-collection photo (a class whose existence is now sworn-testimony-anchored, see §3.4), but the specific transfer is unconfirmed.
4. **TriggerSmart safety-position observation (T4)** — enthusiast-level question about whether the rifle could be operated with the safety positioned as shown under the low-mounted scope; flagged as untested speculation, not evidence of inauthenticity.
5. **Exhibit-level confirmation** — certified transcript and exhibit index from the Fourth District Court would allow Tier 1 anchoring of what was admitted and displayed July 9, 2026. Note: pool photography of exhibits from July 9 exists; on July 10 (Day 5), following the unapproved camera capture of a note exhibit on July 9, Judge Graf barred the media camera from recording evidence going forward, so exhibit imagery from Day 5 onward is restricted.
6. **Image-content verification** — the September 2025 image's depicted details (box material, ground surface) are unverified pending direct examination of an archived copy; current descriptions rest on researcher recollection and commentator characterization.
