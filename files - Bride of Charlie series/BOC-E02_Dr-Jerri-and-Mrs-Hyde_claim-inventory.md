## 3. "What the elites believe" (01:47–~09:00) [3]

Worldview-establishment segment. Owens asserts, in sequence:

- Elites practice occult "sex magic"; Aleister Crowley as the archetype; the 1923 Cefalù (Sicily) death of Raoul Loveday, presented (via producer Ashley) with a story that Loveday grew fainter until he "completely disappeared" during an invisibility working and was buried in an empty coffin, with the official cause (intestinal inflammation) framed as cover. [See Verification Note V-1.]
- Crowley was friends with Pauline Robinson, who gave birth to **Barbara Bush** roughly 8.5 months after spending time with him — presented as "the source of the rumors" that Barbara Bush was Crowley's child. Owens frames the acquaintance as historical fact and lets the paternity implication run. [V-2: long-circulating internet claim without credible documentary support.]
- **Operation Paperclip** was "not about rescuing... scientists" but about rescuing "wealthy occult perverts like Sigmund Freud." [V-3: factually impossible as stated — Freud died in London in September 1939; Paperclip began in 1945.]
- Jack Parsons (JPL founder, Crowley protégé) as proof elite "geniuses" are manufactured; skepticism of Musk and Gates as genuine geniuses; Gates-Epstein reference.
- Core thesis: the military keeps particular families in power — "eugenics at the top and social engineering at the bottom"; society is steered by psychological operations.

### Flag F-1 — direct assassination-orchestration assertion (~08:54–10:02)

Owens asks why "the military" **chose to have the public watch Charlie's execution**, suggesting alternatives (poisoning, staged accident) were available, and answers that the public spectacle was engineered to harness emotion behind Erika ("She's the widow... that's why they keep saying... she's a grieving widow"). She also cites her sit-down with Erika: emotion arose only about Erika's ailing mother, never about Charlie.

**Classification:** Tier 4 assertion; no evidence offered. **Significance for the record:** this is a categorical escalation from Episode 1's closing disclaimer ("I didn't accuse her of killing Charlie Kirk" — BOC-E01 §4.5b). In Ep 2 the assassination is asserted to be a military-orchestrated public spectacle, with Erika as its intended beneficiary. The two postures are in tension within Owens' own series, ~24 hours apart.

### Recap of Ep 1 "military connections" (~10:02–11:20) [3]

Owens restates from Ep 1: Erika born at Good Samaritan Hospital on "either the 20th or the 22nd" of November 1988 ("depends on who's telling the story"); the hospital was "gifted by the Marine Corps after they were caught experimenting on human brains" [V-4: unverified]; grandfather "Carl Kenneth" served in the Army Corps of Engineers, which Owens glosses as sometimes "engineering society"; Kent and Lori married in Marblehead, Massachusetts, "birthplace of Marine Corps aviation," with Owens stating she cannot find any proof the union took place — "in the '80s... the '90s... or at all."

## 4. "Dr. Frantzve" (9:14–~28:00) — the Jerri Frantzve biography [3]

Owens' stated purpose: profile **Kent Frantzve's first wife**, "Dr. Jerri Frantzve" (transcript variants normalized), whom she calls one of the most compelling characters in the story — powerful, connected, and destined to "plug right into" Erika's life. Owens says she died about two years ago (~2024) in Arizona and kept the Frantzve name, and that she and Kent remained on good terms and did business together.

Itemized claims (all Owens assertions unless noted; none independently verified in this sweep — **Tier 4 pending document checks**, Open Items 3–6):

1. Born "Jerri Pitz" (spelling uncertain), 1943, into a strict Mormon family from Arizona; raised in Indianapolis.
2. At 14, ran away to marry; altered her birth certificate and aged it with Clairol; married **Carl Pangerl** (spelled out on air: P-A-N-G-E-R-L), five or six years older, who Owens says later became a church bishop (transcript: "Roman Church"; context suggests Mormon — unresolved). Owens reads from a **1981 newspaper article** in which Frantzve recounted this herself (straight-A student; feared a C in algebra; loved her boyfriend; lied about her age). Owens characterizes the episode as child rape and criticizes the article's romanticized retelling, arguing such a person shouldn't hold power over children. [The 1981 article is a locatable Tier 3 artifact — Open Item 4.]
3. Mother before her peers finished high school; divorced before 18; became a bookkeeper; Owens speculates (explicitly as speculation) her clients must have been important.
4. 1965: married **Kenneth Himsel** (spelling uncertain), a captain in the Indiana National Guard who later became a major general; earned a psychology bachelor's; by 1970 (age 27) a social worker in Indiana.
5. Owens links Indiana to MKUltra ("major hub"; universities involved; LSD manufactured there for experiments) and says she distrusts any military-connected 1970s psychologist. She acknowledges on air: "I get it, I'm a conspiracy theorist." [V-5.]
6. 1971: moved to Chicago; worked ~3 years for **Quaker Oats** in "marketing." Owens then invokes the Quaker Oats radioactive-oatmeal experiments on institutionalized boys (with the Atomic Energy Commission), asks whether Frantzve "played a part," and answers that "we're never going to know" because MKUltra files were destroyed — she says by the military in 1975. [V-6, V-7: the Fernald experiments are real but chronologically and geographically disconnected from a 1971–74 Chicago marketing job; MKUltra destruction was ordered in 1973 by CIA leadership.]
7. Married **Kent Frantzve** while at Quaker Oats (she being a few years older); completed master's and doctorate; the couple moved to **Paradise Valley, Arizona**, then divorced amicably.
8. Post-doctorate: **Director of Employee Relations for DuPont** — glossed by Owens as finding "the psychopaths" to head elite-run corporations; she ties this to the DuPont–Conoco merger, which she dates to 1983 and credits to Reagan/Bush deregulation [V-8: the merger closed in 1981], and draws a Wilmington, Delaware connection to her earlier "Egyptian planes" thread.
9. 1983: "awarded" a **gender clinic / Gender Research Center at Radford University**; "giving lessons to the military." Owens' fraud allegation: a Radford report on Native American/Appalachian subjects lists five authors — she claims **Marjorie Wheyland** (spelling uncertain) is Frantzve's mother and **Richard Caster** her nephew, i.e., undisclosed family co-authorship ("Mormon Mafia," in her phrase). She concludes Frantzve is a "fraud, capital F." [Open Item 5: the report and center are checkable through Radford archives.]
10. **John Money link:** Owens claims Frantzve was friends and colleagues with **Dr. John Money** and "brings over" Money when the Radford gender center opens. A producer clip then recounts the David Reimer case (Money's twins experiment; Reimer's later suicide; abuse allegations). [V-9: the Reimer case summary is broadly consistent with the documented record; the claimed Frantzve–Money professional relationship is unverified.]
11. Teases: Frantzve "gets involved in Eastern Europe" (bridging to the Romania thread), and — separately — "Tyler Bowyer, I'm speaking to you... I know about your time in Romania. I've got the receipts." [Tier 4 tease; no evidence shown in this episode.]

## 10. Methodological assessment (analyst inference — labeled)

1. **Escalation across 24 hours:** Ep 1 closes disclaiming any murder accusation; Ep 2 asserts a military-orchestrated public execution engineered to elevate Erika (F-1). The series' epistemic posture is not stable episode-to-episode.
2. **Self-undermining sourcing:** F-2 (abuse allegation) is presented with an explicit no-evidence admission plus a stated predisposition to believe. Owens also self-identifies on air as a conspiracy theorist (§4.5) while asserting "100%" confidence in anonymous financial claims (§7.3).
3. **Named private individuals:** the episode names several private or deceased individuals (Jerri Frantzve, Carl Pangerl, Kenneth Himsel, Phil Bliss, Jill Kessler, Susan Silverstein) with unverified and in some cases stigmatizing characterizations. This file records those claims as claims; nothing herein corroborates them.
4. **Checkable vs. uncheckable:** the few externally checkable anchors (V-table) skew wrong on dates/attribution; the load-bearing claims (Frantzve→Tesseract design role; $10M missing; Bowyer/Romania) rest entirely on unnamed sources or asserted-but-unshown documents.
5. **Case-file relevance:** F-1 is a new datapoint for the actor map — Owens is now on record (Feb 26, 2026) asserting military orchestration of the assassination as public spectacle. This contradicts no evidence in the criminal record because it engages none of it.
