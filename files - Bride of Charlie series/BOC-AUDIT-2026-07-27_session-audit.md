## PART 8 — SESSION EXTENSION: COURT-RECORD ACQUISITION (added 2026-07-27, after the original close)

The session did not end at Part 7. Its **item 2** — the Fourth District Court docket pull — was executed the same day, and it changed the picture again.

**What happened.** Utah XChange proved **paywalled** ($10 one-time or $25 + $40/month) and was excluded outright — no account created, no fee incurred, because that is a user spending decision. But the filings in this case are **public** (a motion to seal was denied), and two **Tier 1 primary documents** were obtained free through outlet mirrors and banked with SHA-256 provenance: the defense bullet-jacket motion (served 2026-01-09, 19pp + 2 exhibits) and the redacted 2025-10-24 sealed-motion-hearing transcript (97pp). Full detail in **CR-ACQ-2026-07-27**.

**The finding that matters — as corrected in CR-ACQ v1.1.** The ATF laboratory report of 2025-09-17 reaches **two conclusions on two different items**. The **fired cartridge case (Exhibit 2) was positively identified as having been fired in the Exhibit 1 rifle — `source identification`**, the strongest positive conclusion in the DOJ framework. The **bullet jacket fragment recovered at autopsy (6A) was inconclusive**, and the four lead fragments were unsuitable for comparison. The rifle is a **Mauser model 98, .30-06, s/n 8863**, test fired and operable. This is the first hard forensic datum the library holds on the shot itself.

**D-48 — assigned, resolved, and then RE-RESOLVED the same session.** The first resolution read the claim as verifying **for** Owens. That was wrong, and it was wrong for an instructive reason: it rested on the ATF report's language **as quoted in the defense motion**, which quoted accurately but only the part bearing on its own subject. Once Exhibit 2 was read directly, the omitted `source identification` appeared and the resolution flipped. D-48 now resolves **against Owens' published episode description**, which omits the identification and so misrepresents the report as a whole — while the **episode itself is expressly not resolved against** (a 68-minute broadcast may have covered it; unknown), and the bullet-fragment leg stays **confirmed for her**. Ep 319 is promoted to the top of the inventory queue as the decisive artifact.

**The methodological finding, which outlasts the case detail.** **Selective quotation by an adversarial party is a distinct sourcing hazard** — reliable as to the words quoted, unreliable as to what the source document establishes. What kept it from hardening was the project's own discipline: the finding was tiered **T1 quoting T1**, explicitly refused promotion to "read directly," and named direct reading as open item #1. Closing that item reversed the finding within hours. This is the V-10 lesson with a different source — there a **claimant**, here an **adversarial filing** — and it is now a standing rule in CR-ACQ §0.

**Residual specific to this extension.** `poppler` was installed with the user's authorization, so Exhibit 2 is now **read directly** and the tiering gap is closed. **Preliminary-hearing Days 2–5 are banked** (~878k chars, ~5,900 timestamped turns) but **entirely unread** — acquisition is not comprehension, and nothing in the library may cite them for content yet. **Day 1 was not found** (four URL variants, all 404). The **ruling** on the bullet-fragment motion was not obtained, and the State's response to it is absent from the record held — which is why the exculpatory-characterization dispute stays open rather than decided. **No adversarial verification pass has been run on any of this.**
