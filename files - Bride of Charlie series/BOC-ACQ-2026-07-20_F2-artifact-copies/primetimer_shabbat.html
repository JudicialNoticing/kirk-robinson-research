"We are beyond parody" - Candace Owens responds to Blake Neff calling her out over a joke about Erika Kirk's Shabbat Shalom world tour

Candace Owens reacted to Blake Neff calling her out for joking about Erika Kirk's Shabbat Shalom world tour.
posted by Aaratrika Bal
Tuesday 3/3/2026 at 4:10AM EST
  • NATIONAL HARBOR, MARYLAND - MARCH 01: Commentator Candace Owens speaks during CPAC 2019 on March 1, 2019 in National Harbor, Maryland. The American Conservative Union hosts the annual Conservative Political Action Conference to discuss conservative agenda - Source : Getty

    Candace Owens has recently responded to Blake Neff, who called her out for making some comments about Erika Kirk's Shabbat Shalom world tour. On March 2, Neff took to X and claimed that Candace had "aggressively ridiculed" Erika Kirk for using the phase "Shabbat Shalom."

    In the tweet, Blake Neff also referred to Charlie Kirk's posthumous book Stop, in the Name of God, that was released in December 2025. Neff pointed out that Candace apparently claimed that the book was "a fabrication to make Charlie appear more pro-Jewish and pro-Israel than he really was."

    "Charlie was observing his evangelical Christian version of "shabbat" for the entire time I worked under him. As a Catholic who sees Sunday as the Christian sabbath, I would tease Charlie about this!" Blake Neff said.

    Neff further referred to a few screenshots that he shared and wrote,

    "In fact, every week on Friday, around 9-10 pm, our last message from Charlie before he went dark for 24-36 hours was a 'shabbot shalom' in one of our Telegram chats."

    This tweet by Blake garnered massive responses on social media. More than 627K people viewed the post, and over 6.4K ones liked it. Many netizens wrote that they were relieved seeing the justification that Neff gave using proofs. As aforementioned, Candace later reacted to this tweet. She wrote in her tweet posted on March 2,

    "Of everything I have said on the show— Blake Neff has chosen to author a long-winded response to my OBVIOUS joke about Erika's Shabbat Shalom world tour. This is what they NEEDED to clarify. They need the world to know that Charlie loved Shabbat. We are beyond parody."

    For the unversed, the Hebrew phrase literally means "peaceful Sabbath," used from Friday sundown to Saturday nightfall. 

    Blake Neff has previously invited Candace Owens on Charlie Kirk's podcast to clear the air about her accusations against TPUSA

    In December 2025, reports surfaced that suggested that Blake Neff had invited Candace Owens on Charlie Kirk's podcast. Candace has come up with a bunch of allegations and speculations since Charlie was assassinated in September 2025. The invitation came after she accused TPUSA of betraying the late political activist.

    According to The Hindustan Times, this prompted Neff to send an invite to Candace to appear on the podcast. Reports indicated that while Candace initially agreed to be a part, eventually scheduling issues popped up. Candace Owens reportedly said that it was not possible for her to be present in-person for the podcast in Phoenix.

    According to Candace, the podcast episode was scheduled at the same time as her own show. On December 4, Candace tweeted that Neff didn't discuss with her her availability through either e-mails or calls.

    "Kind of weird how you didn’t e-mail or call me to ask about times or availability and chose to instead tweet this confirmation out at midnight. Why am I learning about this on X?" Candace wrote at the time.

    Candace further suggested that while she could not be a part of the podcast episode, she could join the livestream. Neff then took to X to issue a response through another tweet, which read,

    "Yesterday, we made it clear that we would be scheduling a formal response to the allegations and accusations you have made. We've decided that response will be delivered during a livestream on December 15th."

    In the tweet, Blake Neff further wrote that they would continue the show with any involvement of Candace Owens. The tweets have eventually garnered millions of views since the time they were posted on the social media platform. Many netizens had criticized Neff at the time and believed that it wasn't how one should book a guest for a podcast episode.

    In response, Neff wrote that they were not booking a guest when they invited Candace Owens on the podcast. He added that they were "delivering a response to several months of escalating harassment and lies."

    Charlie Kirk was called a "friend of Israel" by Israeli leaders in the past

    According to reports by Al Jazeera, leaders such as Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu and other officials had paid tributes to Charlie Kirk after he was assassinated last year. Just minutes after the news about Charlie's death was announced, Netanyahu tweeted,

    "A lion-hearted friend of Israel, he fought the lies and stood tall for Judeo-Christian civilization. I spoke to him only two weeks ago and invited him to Israel. Sadly, that visit will not take place."

    According to the outlet, Israeli Foreign Minister Gideon Saar also called Charlie an "incredible friend" of Israel. Saar wrote in a social media post that Charlie Kirk portrayed "Judeo-Christian values that unite Israel and America."

    Netizens reacted to Candace Owens' tweet as well, with many claiming that she actually sounded "condescending" when she "joked." Meanwhile, many sided with Candace and had a similar point of view about the late political activist Charlie Kirk.
