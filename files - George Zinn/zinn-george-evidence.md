## 1. Events of September 10, 2025

**1.1 — The false confession at the scene.** Police reports describe Zinn, then 71, beginning to shout that he was the shooter approximately two minutes after the single shot that killed Kirk at Utah Valley University in Orem. He approached UVU Police Det. Michael Dutson, and when asked where the gun was, refused to say and repeated the demand: "I shot him, now shoot me." **[T1 via T3]**
- Deseret News (via Yahoo), Jan. 29–30, 2026: https://www.yahoo.com/news/articles/man-falsely-claimed-shoot-charlie-233127574.html
- KSL TV, Sept. 16, 2025: https://ksltv.com/local-news/utah-man-who-falsely-claimed-he-shot-charlie-kirk-booked-into-jail/819948/

**1.2 — Detention.** The officer found Zinn unarmed but detained him due to his persistent claims and erratic behavior; video of officers carrying him from the scene circulated widely. **[T2/T3]**
- Lynnwood Times, Sept. 16, 2025: https://lynnwoodtimes.com/2025/09/16/george-zinn/
- Newsweek, Sept. 17, 2025: https://www.newsweek.com/who-george-zinn-man-accused-fake-charlie-kirk-confession-2131012

**1.3 — Two detainees, both cleared.** Zinn was one of two people detained that day; the Utah Department of Public Safety identified the second as Zachariah Qureshi. Both were determined to have no connection to the shooting. **[T2]**
- NewsNation, Sept. 16, 2025: https://www.newsnationnow.com/crime/first-man-arrested-charlie-kirk-shooter-escape/
- Newsweek, Sept. 17, 2025 (link above)

**1.4 — Recantation at the police station.** Per the probable cause affidavit (Det. Dutson): at the UVU Police Department, Zinn requested an attorney; Dutson told him he did not believe Zinn was the shooter; Zinn then stated, unprompted, that he did not shoot Kirk and had made the claim to draw attention from the real shooter. **[T1 via T3]**
- Deseret News, Sept. 22, 2025: https://www.deseret.com/politics/2025/09/22/who-is-george-zinn-in-charlie-kirks-murder/

**1.5 — Hospital statements.** Zinn was transported to Utah Valley Regional Medical Center for chest pain. While there, he told the accompanying officer he was glad he had claimed to be the shooter so the real suspect could get away, and that he "wanted to be a martyr for the person who was shot" (affidavit language — note: the filing does not name Kirk in this phrase). **[T1 via T3]**

**1.6 — Statements to federal/state agents; CSAM disclosure.** Per the Utah County Sheriff's Office: at the hospital Zinn spoke with an FBI agent and a Utah State Bureau of Investigation agent, telling them he yelled to let the actual suspect flee and hinder the law enforcement response. When the agents asked to examine his phone, Zinn volunteered that he used it to view child sexual abuse material; agents observed images on the device. **[T2]**

A judge ordered him held without bail on September 15. **[T1 via T3 / T3]**

## 2. CSAM case

**2.1 — Warrant and findings.** After Zinn's spontaneous disclosure (1.6), Utah County Special Victims Unit detectives obtained a search warrant (executed Monday, Sept. 15, 2025) and reported finding more than 20 images of children approximately ages 5–12 in sexualized contexts, plus graphic text threads in which Zinn shared images with others. Charging documents state he represented some images as photos of his daughters. Zinn admitted to investigators that he derived sexual gratification from viewing and sharing the material. **[T2 / T1 via T3]**
- Newsweek, Sept. 17, 2025 (link above)

**2.2 — Charges.** Formally charged Sept. 19, 2025: one count obstruction of justice (second-degree felony as filed) and four counts sexual exploitation of a minor (second-degree felonies). A separate misdemeanor theft case (January 2025) was also pending. **[T1 via T3]**

## 4. Motive: what is established and what is not

**4.1 — Documented self-stated motives (all T1 via T3 or T2):**
1. To draw attention from the real shooter (station statement, affidavit — 1.4)
2. So the real suspect could get away / to hinder the law enforcement response (hospital statements to officer and to FBI/UBI agents — 1.5, 1.6)
3. To be "a martyr for the person who was shot" (affidavit — 1.5)

**4.2 — Internal tension.** Motives (1)/(2) (aiding the shooter's escape) and motive (3) (martyrdom for the victim) point in opposite directions. The record does not reconcile them. No official finding characterizes his "true" motivation.

**4.3 — Contextual evidence (not a finding).** Zinn's decades-long documented pattern — dozens of petty convictions dating to the late 1980s (trespass, theft of services, interfering with police, disturbing the peace); the 2013 marathon bomb-threat email days after the Boston bombing (pleaded to a reduced Class A misdemeanor); a 2019 arrest at an inland port protest; long-standing presence at Utah political events; Salt Lake County DA Sim Gill's pre-2025 characterization of him as an odd but largely harmless gadfly — supports, but does not establish, an attention-seeking/mental-instability explanation. **[T3; Gill characterization T2 via T3]**
- Newsweek, Sept. 17, 2025 (link above)
- Lynnwood Times, Sept. 16, 2025 (link above)
- Yahoo News Canada, Sept. 16, 2025 (Gill quote via Salt Lake Tribune; link above)

**4.4 — Unverified 2013 disposition detail.** The claim that Zinn's 2013 plea included 24 months' probation and a court-ordered mental health evaluation/treatment appears only in Tier 4 sources in current research (ZeroHedge, Sept. 17, 2025: https://www.zerohedge.com/news/2025-09-17/curious-case-george-zinn). The reduced-misdemeanor plea itself is established at T3. **Status: reported, unverified at T1–T3.** Resolution path: 2013 Third District Court record.

**4.5 — Competency.** No competency finding against Zinn appears in the record; at the December 17, 2025 hearing, defense counsel represented that he remained competent to proceed. **[T1 via T3]** (ABC4, Dec. 17, 2025, link above)

**Bottom line:** His stated motive is documented; whether it reflects coherent actual reasoning is not established and is registered as an open question, not resolved editorially.

## 5. The "paid to yell" claim (Discrepancy Register D5)

**5.1 — Origin.** The claim traces to Candace Owens, who has stated — in September 2025 broadcasts and again in the Shawn Ryan Show interview #318 (July 2, 2026) — that she has it on authority from unnamed hospital staff that Zinn told nurses he was paid to make the false confession but did not know the source of the money. **[T4 — anonymous sources relayed by a named commentator]**
- Shawn Ryan Show #318 transcript: https://singjupost.com/transcript-of-candace-owens-interview-shawn-ryan-show-srs-318/
- Alternate transcript: https://podcasts.happyscribe.com/the-shawn-ryan-show/318-candace-owens-the-most-sued-woman-in-american-media
- Owens X post referencing her Zinn timeline coverage, Sept. 2025: https://x.com/RealCandaceO/status/1970591136193323290

**5.2 — Corroboration status: none found.** The claim does not appear in the probable cause affidavit as reported, the sheriff's office statements, the charging documents as reported, the plea, or the sentencing record; no news organization has independently corroborated it as of July 13, 2026. This is an absence-in-the-secondary-record finding (see access-path caveat), not an affirmative disproof.

**5.3 — Consistency check.** Facts weighing against the claim: the sheriff's office no-collusion statement (1.8); the FBI acted-alone finding; and the absence of any conspiracy or accomplice count across the full life of the case through sentencing. Note (logic correction from audit): the *third-degree* classification of the obstruction conviction carries no weight here — that reduction was bargained. The probative absence is of conspiracy-type charges at any degree, at any stage.

**5.4 — Register entry.** Claim: PAID FALSE CONFESSION. Status: **Tier 4, uncorroborated, inconsistent with (though not strictly excluded by) the official record.** Any future corroboration path runs through: hospital-staff witnesses on the record, the full affidavit, or investigative-file disclosures.

## Discrepancy Register

**D1 — Obstruction plea type: "guilty" vs. "no contest."**
Best-supported account: **no contest** to third-degree obstruction; **guilty** to two exploitation counts. Convergent in KSL, Salt Lake Tribune, Deseret News, The Hill/NewsNation, Breitbart-via-Hill. Outlet errors registered: ABC4 headline ("pleads guilty to obstruction of justice"), KUTV and ABC3340 body text ("pleaded guilty to third-degree felony obstruction"). Classified as reporting imprecision, not a genuine record conflict. **Resolution path:** plea agreement / change-of-plea minutes.

**D2 — Sequence of Zinn's admissions (station vs. hospital).**
The sheriff's-office narrative (T2) has Zinn uncooperative until agreeing to speak at the hospital. The affidavit narrative (T1 via T3) has the core recantation and "draw attention from the real shooter" statement occurring at the UVU Police Department, before transport — with the "glad" and "martyr" statements, and the FBI/UBI interview, at the hospital. Both are official accounts; the affidavit is the higher-tier source and is adopted in §1, but the tension is preserved here. **Resolution path:** affidavit full text.

**D3 — Plea/sentencing hearing date: Jan. 28 vs. Jan. 29, 2026.**
Salt Lake Tribune photo captions conflict internally: some (syndicated in Deseret/ABC4 pieces) date the arraignment "Wednesday, Jan. 28, 2026"; the Tribune's own sentencing article captions it "Thursday, Jan. 29, 2026." Article body text across Deseret, KSL, and ABC4 consistently says "Thursday"; Jan. 29, 2026 was a Thursday. Best-supported reading: **hearing Thursday, Jan. 29, 2026**, with the Jan. 28 captions likely caption errors — flagged, not resolved. **Resolution path:** 4th District Court docket.

**D4 — "Admitted" language vs. no-contest plea.**
KSL reports Zinn "admitted" on the hearing date that he intended to hinder or delay the investigation by providing false information — phrasing in tension with a no-contest plea, which entails no admission. Likely explanation: loose description of the factual basis recited at the plea colloquy. Registered as wording-level conflict. **Resolution path:** plea colloquy transcript.

**D5 — Owens "payment" claim.** See §5. Tier 4; uncorroborated; origin identified (Owens, unnamed hospital staff).
