PC STATEMENT


On September 10,2025 11:00AM I, Detective Dutson was on duty working an                                          Event at Utah
Valley University. I was stationed at the back of the
venue to stop anyone from entering the event. At about 12:20PM I heard what
sounded like a single gunshot. I immediately observed patrons attending the
event starting to run in every direction. I observed a person lying on the
ground and it appeared people were attending to the person. I started to work my
way through the crowd that was running from the area. I was approached by an
elderly male that was later identified as George Zinn. The suspect immediately
started yelling at me "I shot him now shoot me. I could see both of his hands
and could not see a weapon. He again stated, "I shot him now shoot me". I then
advised him that he was under arrest, and he was placed in handcuffs.
I asked the suspect where the gun was, and he stated I am not going to tell you.
I did a quick search of his person and did not feel or find a weapon.
I then started to escort George to a patrol car that was at the back of the
venue. He continued to state that he shot the individual and to just shoot him. He was
placed in a patrol car. Officer Wallace and I transported him to the Utah Valley
University Police Department.

When we arrived at the Police Department I attempted to interview the
suspect. Before advising him of his right, He stated he wanted an
attorney. At that point I advised George I did not think he was the one who did
the shooting. But since he told me he wanted an attorney I could not talk to
him. At that point he advised I am already going to be in trouble for saying I
shot him. Even if I did not. I did not ask him anymore questions at that point.
George stated on his own that he did not shoot the individual.
He stated he did it to draw attention from the real shooter.

Due to a medical condition George was transported to the hospital. An Officer
remained with George for the ambulance ride to the Hospital and stayed with him
in the Emergency Room. During this time George made statements to the Officer
that he was glad he said he shot the individual so the real suspect could get
away. He also stated to Officers that he wanted to be a martyr for the person who was shot.                        George was
admitted to the hospital. The hospital stated they would
contact law enforcement when he was released. On September 15,2025 he was
released from the hospital. The Utah County Sheriff Officer transported him to
the Utah County Jail. I arrived at the jail and booked him on the charges below.

The suspect while at the even made statement that he did the shooting which
delayed and took up Law Enforcement resources into the investigation.
Therefore, George Zinn is being booked onto the Utah County Jail for 76-8-306
Obstruction of justice and 2nd degree felony. This was committed during the act
of the murder at Utah Valley University on September 10,2025.
