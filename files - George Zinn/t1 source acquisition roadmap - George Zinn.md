## Explicitly NOT obtainable through these routes (managing expectations on D5)

- **Hospital records / staff identities** (Utah Valley Regional Medical Center): protected health information; no public-records path. The Owens payment claim cannot be tested through hospital records by any route available here.
- **FBI interview records (302s)**: FOIA-eligible in principle, but routinely withheld under exemption 7(A) while any related matter (State v. Robinson) remains pending; expect denial or heavy redaction until final adjudication. Long-horizon item only.
- **Grand jury / prosecution work product**: not GRAMA-subject.

**D5 realistic test surface:** A3/E1 (affidavit — omission test), B5 (body-cam — scene utterances), B7 (UBI interview record — direct but likely protected), C1 (colloquy — omission test). If all four are silent, D5 remains Tier 4/uncorroborated with an expanded omission record; none of them can affirmatively disprove a claim about statements to nurses.
